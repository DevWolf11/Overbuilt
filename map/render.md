# Rendering (js/render3d.js, js/parts3d.js), with the bits of world.js, flight.js, combat.js, mp.js and parts.js that feed the 3D view

Every craft is drawn by Parts3D.VesselMesh (parts3d.js:1705). Each part goes through recipe() (1241), which picks from def.body3d, PART_RECIPE (parts.js:969), lathe or extrude. A BUILD[recipe] (647) then returns a node: {pieces: [[geometry, kind]], subs, anim}. The node is cached by type, flip and (for wings only) skin (1249), and instance() (1481) turns it into one mesh per material kind, with materials owned by the part. Sub-node roles (spin, spinRev, droop, store, hinge, ruMove, and single-slot roles such as yaw, pitch, pivot and lid) are animated each frame in VesselMesh.update (1779-1854) from part state: p.spin, p.aimYaw/aimEl, p.deflect, p.deployed, p.ammo, p.retracted. Textured materials reuse the 2D drawPart art (paint 1259, textureFor 1305).

Flight: renderFlight (render3d.js:2398) uses a floating origin at F.camAbs (the viewed craft, not the camera). The camera always orbits that craft and lookAt(0,0,0), at a distance set by the 2D zoom F.scale through the constant TANH (FOV 50). There is no gun or cockpit camera. Vessels and multiplayer ghosts go through the same path (syncVessels 2772). The ground is one double-buffered patch of about 22 km half-span. Cells are about 1 m under the focus but about 44 m (high) / 66 m (low) 200 m away and 100-150 m at 1 km, while physics uses a 2 m lattice. Roads and fields are vertex colours only, and trees and buildings are visual only.

Effects are FX particles (world.js:32) drawn as two instanced quad meshes (1100 each, render3d.js:2253), plus tracers and pooled missile, rocket and bomb models (syncCombat 3112). There is no muzzle flash on turrets, no driving dust and no decals.

Cost: a stand-in tank (chassis, cab, 6 wheel parts with tracks, manned turret) is 94 meshes, all shadow casters, about 20k triangles. The FS-16 is 58 and the Buggy 25. A wheel part with a track costs 10 meshes, and shadows draw them again on high quality.

## Key facts
- Recipe choice: def.body3d, then 'box' for side separators, then PART_RECIPE[shape], then 'lathe' if the shape is in LATHE_SHAPES, else 'extrude'. A new recipe registers in PART_RECIPE or def.body3d. (js/parts3d.js:1241-1245, js/parts.js:969-972)
- Models are cached under type + '|f' + ('|s:skin' for SKIN_GEO wing/biplane only). Geometry may depend only on def, flip and (for wings) skin. Per-part motion must go through sub-node roles in update(), never into geometry. (js/parts3d.js:1248-1253)
- node(list, subs, extra) merges pieces per material kind, so each node costs one draw per kind. (js/parts3d.js:618-623)
- instance() collects roles: spin goes to rig.spins (and sets rig.axis, one axis per rig, the last one wins); spinRev goes to rig.revs; droop, store, hinge and ruMove go to lists; every other role does rig[role] = g (a single slot). A twinZ pair builds the model in two holders, so for yaw, pitch, pivot or lid only the last holder's copy is animated. (js/parts3d.js:1490-1501)
- Turret animation: rig.yaw.rotation.x = p.aimYaw and rig.pitch.rotation.z = p.aimEl. Yaw turns about the part's own x (mount) axis and pitch about its z axis. (js/parts3d.js:1804)
- Small turret model: base on the +x mount side, a 'yaw' dome node, a 'pitch' twin-gun node. The manned turretL (mannedTurret) has a yaw collar and glazed dome, and twin guns on a pitch node pivoting at the trunnion pos [-0.04, 0.15, 0], with a 0.2 m skirt reaching into the host. (js/parts3d.js:882-892, 627-643)
- Aim angles are computed in combat.turretStep: 2.5 rad/s slew, yaw about the part's x axis, frames turned by p.rot & 3, parked at 0,0 when touching the ground. turretFire adds no muzzle flash FX, unlike fireGun. (js/combat.js:355-375, 377-394, 259)
- Wheels: wheelNode (cached by r and W; 4 kinds: tyre, steelLight, orange hub, steelDark spokes) plus wheelPair, which gives one wheel or a cosmetic pair at z = ±track on 'spin' subs about z. The wheel recipe adds a strut and axle: 10 meshes per carWheel part. (js/parts3d.js:597-615, 987-992)
- Wheel spin comes from physics: every wheel gets w.spin -= (vt / r)·dt from the hull's forward speed vt, so all wheels turn alike and none turn during a pivot turn or under braking. (js/vessel.js:1049)
- Spin animation uses numeric p.spin, else fspin += throttle·anim.rate·dt. The blur disc opacity follows the measured rate and the disc is not drawn while its opacity is 0. (js/parts3d.js:1784-1797)
- Other animated roles: pivot.rotation.z = p.deflect; hinge rotation[axis] = open·clamp(p.deployed); store visibility from p.ammo, alternating sides; a retractable wheel hides its root; the lid is hidden once deployed. (js/parts3d.js:1798-1821)
- Material kinds (KINDS): hull/metal/gold/shield/sep take the 2D art texture; vc/vcCamo use vertex colours; mil, olive, sand and the other BODY_KINDS take the paint's base colour; skin is a camo panel in metres; glass and goldglass are transparent; lamp and tail are basic materials; disc is the blur texture. (js/parts3d.js:13-53, 60)
- makeMat applies the skin: map kinds get the skin painted into textureFor; BODY_KINDS get S.base; skin/vcCamo get camoMap(S, camoAt). It sets envMap, userData.op and userData.c0 (the heat-glow base colour). (js/parts3d.js:1335-1356)
- The 2D art becomes the 3D texture: paint() draws drawPart() with PartPaint.flat = true at 64 px/m (16-512 px, power of two), with 8 offset bleed copies, onto a dummy part (spin 0, deflect 0). us4body textures add the plan view from drawPartTop. Textures are cached by type, flip, skin and camo place, and only placed camo textures are disposed. (js/parts3d.js:1259-1278, 1305-1317, 89)
- camoMap clones a shared texture per offset (t.clone(); offset.set). This is the precedent for a per-rig scrolling track texture. (js/parts3d.js:1322-1331)
- place() runs every sync: root at (p.x, p.y, z), rotation.z = quarter turns (+π for pushers), z-mirror for ZMIRROR recipes on the left side, twin holders at ±z. Moving a part never rebuilds it. (js/parts3d.js:1678-1685)
- VesselMesh.sync adds missing parts, prunes gone ones (freeRig disposes per-rig materials, plumes and pylons), rebuilds pylons on a layout signature change, keeps this.cmd = v.cmd, then runs update() per rig. (js/parts3d.js:1758-1777, 1667-1674)
- Car part space: forward +x, up +y, right +z (pilotFrame). VEHICLE_KINDS.car has launchRot 0, so the hangar shows the blueprint upright as drawn. (js/vessel.js:103, js/parts.js:28)
- Renderer: logarithmic depth buffer; flight camera FOV 50, near 0.05, far 5e12; hangar bcam near max(0.02, dist/2000). (js/render3d.js:473, 485-486, 2970)
- FOV is a constant: FOV = 50 and TANH = tan(25°). It is reused for camera distance, glow point scale, planet pixel size, vessel visibility, hangar markers, project/metresPerPixelAt, designPoint and surfaceHit, and globe.js hardcodes 50° too. (js/render3d.js:8, 2416, 2517, 2537, 2791, 2966, 3014, 3330, 3388, 3401, 3413; js/globe.js:78, 96)
- The flight camera orbits the focus at dist = (H/2)/F.scale/TANH, follows the craft's heading (followCam eases at 2.2/s), is clamped 2 m above the rendered ground and carriers, and always does lookAt(0,0,0). A fluttering wing shakes it. There is no cockpit, free or gunner camera. (js/render3d.js:2382-2395, 2412-2435)
- Default camera offsets by kind (CAM0): car [0, 0.22], behind. Zoom: F.scale is clamped to 0.00005..80 px/m and set at launch to at most 40. The closest camera is (H/2)/80/0.466, about 10.7 m on an 800 px-tall screen, so it depends on screen height. (js/render3d.js:2381, js/flight.js:59, 1134)
- Floating origin: Flight.updateCamera sets F.camAbs to the viewed craft's absolute position and F.viewQ by slerp. renderFlight copies them, and localWorld/spaceWorld carry viewQ. Content goes in at abs − camAbs, computed in doubles. (js/flight.js:1454-1468, js/render3d.js:2406-2410)
- Vessels and multiplayer ghosts: syncVessels builds a VesselMesh per F.vessels entry (radial only for rockets), hides one beyond 50 km and under 1 px, places root = abs − camAbs with q = v.q and group offset −com, and disposes it the first frame the vessel is missing or dead. (js/render3d.js:2772-2804)
- Ghost parts get deflect, firing, ab, retracted, sweep, aimYaw/aimEl, ammo, deployed, used and gunOn from 'pv' rows at 5 Hz (MP_PV_MS 200), with no smoothing. Rows only cover wing, thrust, weapon, chute or retractable-wheel parts, so a remote car's wheels never turn (the ghost isn't stepped). (js/mp.js:17, 627-638, 793-808)
- Terrain patch: a (2n+1)×(2m+1) grid with n,m = 96,56 on high and 64,36 on low, spacing sign(u)|u|^2.2·span. At ground level span is about 21.7-24.8 km, so cells are about 1 m at the centre, 44 m (high) / 66 m (low) at 200 m and 100-150 m at 1 km. It is rebuilt in the background after moving 1.2% of span (about 260 m). (js/render3d.js:897-898, 954-955, 2618-2647)
- Physics ground is Terrain.surfaceAt: a 2 m lattice of exact heights, with the 2D profile on the track. The renderer's meshHeight bilinearly samples the drawn patch. Vessels sit on physics ground while scenery sits on meshHeight. (js/terrain.js:603-627, js/render3d.js:1180-1193)
- Ground detail is a 12 m repeating detail plus normal map. Farmland, hedges, urban blocks, streets and roads are vertex colours that fade by cell size. There is no road geometry and no decal system. (js/render3d.js:10, 809-813, 1018-1059)
- Trees, rocks and buildings are instanced and visual only (no physics or combat collision). Tree cells are 22 m on high and 34 m on low, so positions differ by quality setting. They sit on the rendered patch, out to clamp(3·dist + 600, 800, 2400) m. The Faroes and Shetland (the spawn area) have no trees. (js/render3d.js:1218-1262, 2685-2707)
- FX particles (smoke, spark, flash, ring, fire, glint, plasma in 2D only) are capped at 900 (high) / 350 (low) by dropping the oldest. Emitters: puff, sparks, explosion (about 58 particles), splash. 3D draws two instanced quad meshes of 1100 (smoke with normal blending and fog; fire additive), unsorted. (js/world.js:32-95, js/render3d.js:2253-2311)
- Dust: Flight.groundDust(v, count, ...) throws smoke along the ground at the craft's surface spot. It is used only by engine thrust near the ground and rotor wash (active craft, high quality). Nothing makes dust from driving. (js/flight.js:915-921, 952-966, 969-975)
- Combat visuals: tracer LineSegments and head points (700), pooled missile/rocket/bomb models scaled by s.small/s.big, and range-target models including a box 'tank', an 'aa' gun whose turret wobbles while firing, and a 'sam'. (js/render3d.js:3070-3110, 3112-3170)
- Engine light: one PointLight at the active craft's weighted exhaust. Shadows (high only) use a camera fitted to the active craft (half-size max(25, ext·2.5)), and the shadow map isn't redrawn while the Sun lights nothing. (js/render3d.js:2805-2816, 2510-2529)
- Hangar: draws only while something moves (bv.moving, bc.auto, edit, drag, bd.anim). The VesselMesh is rebuilt only when the per-part key id/type/flip/skin changes. bd.anim stays true only for always-spinning parts (the rotodome). (js/render3d.js:2904-2926)
- Measured headless (VesselMesh traversal): Buggy 25 meshes / 4.3k triangles; FS-16 58 / 21.7k; stand-in tank (Buggy + 6 carWheel + turretL) 94 / 20k. Every mesh casts shadows, so high quality draws them twice. carWheel = 10 meshes, turretL = 9. (scratch measurement via Parts3D.VesselMesh in a Playwright page (test/lib/browser.mjs harness))
- Performance levers: Settings.fps (a 60 cap on touch devices), calm frames at 10 fps when paused or under a dialog, adaptive pixel ratio (0.55-1, base min(DPR, 1.75) on high), and Render3D.skipDraw for tests. Render3D.info().calls reflects only the last render() pass. (js/main.js:52-116, js/render3d.js:3030-3049, 3283-3284)
- A tap or click in the 3D flight view calls Render3D.pickPart, which uses the part (usePart) or switches to another vessel. (js/flight.js:1175-1180, js/render3d.js:3332-3348)
- 2D art: the wheel's spokes turn by p.spin. The turret is static in 2D (no aim drawn). Both 2D fallback and 3D textures come from drawPart0's switch on def.shape. (js/parts.js:2104-2116, 2532-2541, 1730-1734)

## Extension points
- Tank hull, track, turret and gun models @ js/parts3d.js BUILD (647), registered via PART_RECIPE (parts.js:969) or def.body3d (recipe 1241): Write BUILD.trackUnit / tankTurret / tankGun(d, flip, skin) returning node([[geo, kind]...], subs, {anim}) in part space (origin at the part centre, x and y as in 2D, z across). A flat hull can use the default extrude of the 2D outline (768-771) with def.depth; sloped armour can use d.facets or a loft. Use 'mil' or 'olive' (BODY_KINDS, take paint) for armour and 'tyre', 'steelDark' or 'gunmetal' for fittings. Hulls that stand out of the 2D outline need CLASH_PAD/CLASH_BOXED in parts.js.
- Turret traverse and gun elevation @ js/parts3d.js:1498 (rig[role]) and 1804; combat.js:355-394 sets p.aimYaw/aimEl: Reuse role 'yaw' (about the part's local x, the mount axis) and a nested 'pitch' (about local z) with a pos at the trunnion, as mannedTurret does. To mount on a car hull (+x forward) the part needs rot 3 so its +x points down; combat already turns its frame by p.rot & 3. For a turret that is a twinZ pair, or several guns on one part, change instance() to collect yaw/pitch into arrays.
- Road wheels spinning, per side for skid steering @ js/parts3d.js:611-615 (wheelPair), 1784-1797 (spin update); js/vessel.js:1049 (spin source): Keep the 'spin' role with axis 'z'. Tag each spin group with its side (from its pos z sign or the holder's userData.side) and drive it from per-side values (e.g. p.spinL/p.spinR) that physics sets from each track's speed. A ghost has no stepped spin, so fall back to deriving it from ghost velocity in update() or send it in the pv rows.
- Track belt animation @ js/parts3d.js:1323-1331 (camoMap clone with offset, the precedent), freeRig 1667: Option A (cheapest): a 'track' kind whose texture is cloned per rig (Texture.clone shares the image source in r159, so it is uploaded once). In update(), set map.offset.x = (distance travelled / tread pitch) mod 1, and dispose the clone in freeRig. Option B: an InstancedMesh of track links stepped along the run (more draw-call-friendly than per-link meshes, but must be per vessel). Option C: an onBeforeCompile UV scroll with a uniform plus customProgramCacheKey (keep the log-depth chunks). The 2D art should show treads too (drawPart0 switch), because the texture comes from it.
- Gun recoil, hatch and other moving bits @ js/parts3d.js:1494-1498 and update() 1779-1854: Add a role branch, e.g. 'recoil' (a translate along the barrel from time since p.shots changed). Doors and hatches already work with 'hinge' + p.deployed (1811). A spinning SPAA search radar can copy rotodome's anim.always spin (858-864). In the hangar, bd.anim (render3d.js:2925) must cover it or the preview won't redraw.
- Gunner sight (zoomed view down the barrel) @ js/render3d.js:2412-2435 (camera block in renderFlight), FOV/TANH at :8 and every use listed in keyFacts: Add a camera mode: position = the gun's world point (rig.pitch.matrixWorld, or v.toWorld of the muzzle minus camAbs through toView), orientation along the barrel, camera.fov = 50/zoom, then updateProjectionMatrix. TANH must become a variable recomputed from camera.fov, so project()/metresPerPixelAt (used by World.drawOverlay3D, Combat.drawHUD, MPUI) and the vessel and glow pixel scales stay right. The ground clamp (2424-2428) can stay, and near 0.05 with log depth is fine at the muzzle. Expose an API returning the camera's forward ray in sim axes (via toSim) so combat can aim the turret at the screen centre (War Thunder-style mouse aim). For an orbit aim mode, decouple fc.follow from the hull heading (followCam 2382).
- Muzzle flash, blast and driving dust @ js/world.js:32-95 (FX), js/combat.js:259 (fireGun flash), 377 (turretFire), js/flight.js:952 (groundDust): Emit a 'flash' plus a small 'ring' (muzzle blast) and 'smoke' at the muzzle in turretFire or a new tank-gun fire path. Call Flight.groundDust per track or from the hull at speed for dust. Any new particle kind needs a branch in both updateParticles (render3d.js:2287-2294) and FX.draw (world.js:104+), because 2D and 3D must agree.
- Shells, ATGMs, rocket artillery and SAM missiles in flight @ js/render3d.js:3087-3091 (CB.kinds/pool), 3131-3141: Bullets with tracer:true already draw as tracer lines (APFSDS/HE could be bullets with blast). Add CB.kinds entries (e.g. 'shell', 'atgm') and route s.k to them, or reuse 'missile' scaled by s.small (0.35×) or s.big (1.5×).
- Launcher tubes emptying as they fire (missile launchers, SAM, MLRS) @ js/parts3d.js:1805-1809 (store role), BUILD.store 662-705: Put each round on a 'store' sub-node; rig.stores hide in order as p.ammo drops (alternating twin holders). Tubes stay in the parent node so only the rounds vanish.
- Hangar view for ground vehicles @ js/render3d.js:2863-2999, js/parts.js:25-30 (VEHICLE_KINDS): Cars already stand upright (launchRot 0) and rest on the floor via builderLayout. A new 'tank' kind would need a VEHICLE_KINDS entry (launchRot, cats), a CAM0 entry (render3d.js:2381) and a pilot frame (vessel.js:100-106).
- Smoothing remote turret aim @ js/mp.js:804 (onPartVis) or js/parts3d.js:1804: Ease toward the received aimYaw/aimEl (keep a target on the part and lerp with dt in update or poseGhost). Today a ghost's turret steps 5 times a second.
- Seating distant vehicles on the drawn ground @ js/render3d.js:1180-1193 (meshHeight/groundAt), syncVessels 2788-2796: For remote or far vehicles on the ground, optionally offset root.position along local up by (meshHeight − physics height) so they don't float or sink on coarse cells. This is visual only and must not touch the physics.

## Constraints
- Old designs must look exactly the same: existing models (turret, turretL, wheel, carWheel, gear, chassis, cab), the material kinds and the texture art must not change. New looks come from new parts, recipes or kinds (handbook: keep pictures byte-identical where possible).
- Cache keys: a model depends only on def, flip and (for SKIN_GEO) skin. Per-part motion (aim, track scroll, recoil) must be done in update() on sub-nodes or materials, never baked into geometry. A new per-part property that changes geometry needs the model/texture keys, the hangar key (render3d.js:2914-2923), the ghost key (2886) and the pylon signature (parts3d.js:1724-1730) updated.
- 2D and 3D must agree: the 3D texture is the 2D drawPart art (paint 1259), and the 2D fallback (World.render) draws the same parts. New particle kinds need both updateParticles and FX.draw branches. Tracks and turrets need 2D art in drawPart0/partPath0 (the lathe/extrude silhouette comes from partPath0 unless PART_PROFILE overrides it).
- Log depth buffer: custom ShaderMaterials go through smat with the LOGV/LOGF and logdepthbuf chunks; polygonOffset does nothing (use depthBias); an onBeforeCompile hook needs a customProgramCacheKey.
- Floating origin: everything in localWorld is placed at abs − F.camAbs, computed in JS doubles, in sim axes (localWorld carries viewQ). Content that must depth-sort with terrain goes in localWorld, because the far and near passes don't occlude each other.
- parts3d.js must not reference THREE at top level (it loads before three.js; use need()). Duplicate function names inside the IIFE silently override each other. Top-level names are global across files.
- Disposal: models and textures are shared and never disposed per part. freeRig disposes only per-rig materials, plumes and pylons. A per-rig cloned texture (track scroll) must be disposed in freeRig, and Batch.add disposes its input geometry.
- Decorative meshes in a rig must be pickable (userData.part, in rig.meshes) or set raycast = noop. pickHit and the builder's attach rely on this.
- The hangar is drawn on demand. Anything animating in the 3D hangar must keep renderBuilder drawing (bd.anim / bv.moving), and code-made changes must call Builder.redraw().
- Quality-dependent buffers (instance capacities, patch grid) must be reallocated in applyQuality. If you raise a placement cap, raise the InstancedMesh capacity too.
- Battery: the owner tests on phones and tablets. Don't add per-frame work or allocations that don't change the picture. Measure draw calls and CPU before and after. Shadows double every mesh on high, and adaptive resolution only scales pixels, not draw calls.
- Phone portrait layout: camera distance scales with screen height (dist = (H/2)/F.scale/TANH), so third-person framing and any zoomed sight must work at portrait aspect and at the 10.7-21 m camera distances the zoom clamp allows.
- Multiplayer: ghosts are never stepped. The renderer can only show what pv/snapshot rows carry (5 Hz pv, 15 Hz pose). Showing more part state (track speed, per-side wheel spin) is a protocol change that needs a NET_VERSION bump if an older peer would pose or hit a different craft. Peer text must never reach innerHTML.
- Scenery (trees, buildings) depends on quality and on the rendered patch and caps, so it can't serve as shared physical cover in multiplayer without new deterministic placement.

## Risks
- Far vehicles float or sink: physics seats them on a 2 m lattice, but the drawn ground has about 44-66 m cells at 200 m and 100-150 m at 1 km. At War Thunder ranges (0.5-2 km) enemy tanks on hilly ground can look metres above or inside the hill. The player's own tank can show it too up to about 260 m from the patch centre before a rebuild.
- Draw calls: a modest tank-like build is 94 meshes (the FS-16 is 58), mostly road wheels (10 meshes per carWheel part). A 6v6 battle at 100-150 meshes per tank, doubled by shadows on high, could run to 1,500+ calls on phones. A tank road-wheel model needs fewer kinds per wheel (or merged/instanced wheels per side).
- Single-slot roles: rig.yaw, rig.pitch and rig.pivot keep only the last holder's node, so a twinZ turret or gun pair animates only one copy. rig.axis is one per rig, so mixed spin axes in one model break.
- Wheel spin is uniform (vt/r from hull speed): pivot or neutral steering shows no wheel or track motion and both sides turn alike. On ghosts the wheels don't turn at all (not in pv rows, ghost not stepped).
- Ghost turret aim updates at 5 Hz with no smoothing: remote turrets visibly step.
- FOV is a constant (TANH) used in about 12 places plus globe.js. A gunner-sight zoom that changes camera.fov without refactoring breaks project()/metresPerPixelAt (HUD markers, missile markers, MP name tags), glow point sizes and vessel pixel culling.
- The camera always lookAt(0,0,0) on the focus craft and follows its heading. Mouse-aim (camera independent of the hull, turret slaved to the view) needs a new camera path and an exposed aim ray. A click in the 3D flight view already means usePart/switch vessel (flight.js:1175-1180), which conflicts with click-to-fire.
- Particle budget: FX.max is 350 on low and an explosion is about 58 particles; dust, muzzle smoke and smoke screens will evict each other (oldest dropped). Smoke quads are unsorted with normal blending, so dense smoke screens will show sorting artefacts.
- Scenery is visual only and quality-dependent, and the spawn area (Faroes and Shetland) is treeless, so there is no cover for ground combat without new work. Making the current trees physical would desync players on different quality settings.
- A destroyed vessel's VesselMesh is disposed the frame it dies or empties (render3d.js:2801), and parts blown off just vanish with an explosion. There is no persistent burning-wreck model for a knocked-out tank (the range targets have one; players don't).
- Shadow camera is fitted only to the active craft (half max(25 m, 2.5·extent)), so nearby enemy tanks outside that box cast no shadows.
- Per-rig cloned track textures add a material uniform per tank. It is cheap, but must be disposed and must not be shared through the textures cache, or every tank's tracks would scroll together.

## Open questions
- Is the ground update a new vehicle kind (e.g. 'tank', with its own CAM0, pilot frame, VEHICLE_KINDS launchRot and tray categories) or more parts for the existing 'car' kind? The renderer keys camera defaults and radial copies on v.kind.
- Gunner sight: a real 3D camera at the gun with a narrow FOV (needs the TANH refactor and a new camera path), or a third-person camera with a zoomed reticle overlay? Which works better on a phone in portrait?
- Aiming model: War Thunder mouse-aim (camera free of the hull, turret follows the view centre) versus the current heading-following orbit camera plus separate aim controls. How do touch players aim?
- Track visuals: scrolling texture on a belt mesh (cheap) or instanced links (nicer, costlier)? Must the 2D blueprint and the 2D fallback animate treads too?
- Should distant ground vehicles be visually reseated on the drawn terrain (meshHeight) to hide floating and sinking, accepting a small mismatch with where shots hit?
- Is physical cover (trees, buildings, rocks that block movement and shells) in scope? It would need deterministic, quality-independent placement shared with physics and multiplayer.
- Should ghost wheel and track motion and smoothed turret aim come from new pv fields (a NET_VERSION bump) or be derived on the receiver from ghost velocity and interpolation?
- What is the draw-call and frame-time budget per tank on the owner's phone? It sets whether road wheels must be merged or instanced and whether shadows should skip small parts.
