# Parts and the builder (js/parts.js, js/builder.js), with the touchpoints they share with mp.js, main.js, index.html and the tests

PARTS (parts.js:40-816) is a flat catalogue of 257 defs keyed by the type string that saves store. A loop at :817-822 stamps `type` and a default `maxTemp`. Each def carries cat/name/desc/tier/cost/mass and a side-view box w×h (an optional `depth` across z), and a `shape` that picks the 2D outline (partPath0 :1736), the 2D art (drawPart0 :1900), the 3D recipe (LATHE_SHAPES/PART_RECIPE :967-972) and the clash shape (clashShape :1108). Behaviour is opt-in through fields: control/torque, wheel{}, weapon{}, armor, stealth, twinZ, pod, flush and so on.

Ground support is thin today. The 'ground' tab holds four parts: cab, chassis, chassisL and carWheel (:423-430). The car kind (:28) launches from the runway, upright (launchRot 0, +x forward, +y up, +z right). Its tray shows ground, utility, structure and weapons. A wheel part is one axle, with two wheels `track` apart. Driving is a speed controller with front-wheel steering, and mass plays no part in it (vessel.js:972-1050). Weapons fire along the part's own axis in the blueprint plane, so on a car a gun must be turned rot 3 to face forward. The only aiming weapons are 'turret' parts. Their aim is AI-driven, and they hold fire while touching the ground (combat.js:351-375).

The builder handles every kind the same way. Car symmetry mirrors front and back (x→−x), not left and right. The stats warnings for cars amount to "add a cab" and "add driven wheels". The hangar puts every `military` template under "Military aircraft", and the only car template is the Dune Buggy. Battles price designs with designCost against Builder.mp.budget, and MP.designProblems checks them; 'car' is already an allowed PvP kind. Design rows and codes carry only type/x/y/flip/rot/z/att/skin, so any new per-part configuration belongs in defs, not in saves.

## Key facts
- Tray tabs: PART_CATEGORIES (14 entries, ids incl. 'ground' and 'weapons'); def.cat must be one of these ids or the part never shows (js/parts.js:5-20)
- VEHICLE_KINDS: car = {site:'runway', launchRot:0, cats:['ground','utility','structure','weapons']}; heli also lists 'ground'; the other tabs sit behind More (js/parts.js:25-30)
- Ground parts: cab (2.4×1.5, depth 2, control 2, torque 4000, crew 2, aero, breakSpeed 11, $2,500), chassis (4×0.5, depth 2.2, $800), chassisL (6×0.5, depth 2.4, $1,100), carWheel (1.2×1.2, wheel{r .54, drive, speed 38, accel 5, track 2}, breakSpeed 16, $600). Utility 'wheel' (Rover Wheel, speed 12, accel 2.5) is the other driven wheel (js/parts.js:423-430, :110)
- Weapon defs: weapon{kind gun|rocket|missile|bomb|flare|chaff|turret, ammo (counts both copies of a pair), rate, speed, dmg, blast, spread, each (kg per round), range, seek air|ground|radar, radar, cone, accel, burn, vmax, turn, life, glide, cluster, torpedo, label}. Ground-usable stock weapons: gun, cannon (30 mm, blast 3), gatling, gunPods (twin), rocketPods (twin), agm (seek ground, 8 km), harm, cruiseMissile, aam/radarMissiles (seek air), turretL (manned turret, AI), flares, chaff, rwr/rwrPlus, ecm (no weapon block) (js/parts.js:349-420)
- The only armour-like field is def.armor (0..1, only fuselageArmor 0.6); damage is scaled by (1-armor); hit points come from mass and fuel, x1.4 for a control part (maxHp). There is no penetration, angle or thickness model (js/combat.js:37, :47)
- def.crew is informational only: no code outside the PARTS table reads it. torque is summed as reaction-wheel torque; radar, stealth, ecm, irst and rwr are read by combat.js (js/vessel.js:660; js/combat.js:172)
- tier must index TIERS (0-4, rocket-themed names); cost is required (designCost sums def.cost); retired:true removes a part from the tray and R&D but keeps it loadable (js/parts.js:825-831, :1293; js/builder.js:314; js/main.js:337)
- makePart builds {id, type, def, x, y, z, att, skin, flip, fuel(kg), active, deployed, temp}, plus ammo and store for weapons; ids are page-unique (_partSeq) (js/parts.js:838-850)
- Geometry: partDims swaps w/h on odd rot; partPt and partAxis (the part's own +y turned by rot); partRect gives the side-view box; partDepth = def.depth, else wing.span, else 0.15 for fins, 0.4 for ANY wheel (whatever its track), else w (js/parts.js:852-879)
- Depth: zLocked(d) = wing||leg||fin; partDefaultZ = twinZ||0; cleanZ clamps (pairs to [twinMinZ,20], others to ±20); partZ, partZc (0 for pairs), partZs ([-z,z] for pairs), partBoxes (js/parts.js:887-913)
- wheelGeom: wheel centre and radius; a 'gear' shape's wheel sits at the strut's outer end, any other wheel hangs 5% of h below centre. An axle's two wheels sit ±track/2 across z (massZs; parts3d wheel recipe builds a wheelPair with an axle rod) (js/parts.js:917-921; js/vessel.js:17-20; js/parts3d.js:987-992)
- Joints: partsTouch (flush faces within 0.02 m, overlap >0.1, depth overlap); partsMounted (a surface through a hull); pylonLink (old twins with no att); buildAdjacency is O(n²) plus every att link; legacyTwin (att null, at the default spread within 5e-4) (js/parts.js:926-947, :1194-1220, :941)
- Clash: partsClash = box overlap AND shapesClash > 0.02 m, unless exactly one part is flush, a legacy twin meets a non-twin, or the two mix (one is a surface). clashShape is round (lathe), flat (extrude, depth = def.depth||PART_FLAT_DEPTH||min(w,1)), loft, or box (every other PART_RECIPE: cab, chassis, wheel, gun, turret, store...). CLASH_PAD and CLASH_BOXED cover fittings outside the outline (js/parts.js:1100-1125, :1167-1193)
- Probed in Node: cab, chassis, carWheel and turretL all have box clash shapes. Dune Buggy joins: chassis touches cab and both wheels (all in one component); designCost $4,500, 1,260 kg (js/builder.js:136-139 (probe run on the current code))
- 2D drawing: HULLS.cab (driving towards +x) and PART_PROFILE.cab (the 3D extrude outline); drawPart0 cases cab (orange body via bodyGrad, windows, lamps), chassis (bodyFlat), wheel (spokes spin with p.spin), gun (barrels>1: rotary), turret (style 'manned' -> drawMannedTurret); the default case draws a hull-gradient rectangle (js/parts.js:1700-1705, :1063-1068, :2104-2117, :2444-2468, :2470-2486, :2532-2541, :2567)
- Turret orientation: its base is on local +x (the mounting side) and its guns point along local +y. 3D model nodes use role 'yaw' (about part x) and role 'pitch'. AI sets p.aimYaw/p.aimEl and slews at 2.5 rad/s; it only fires when not grounded (contactTime > 0 parks it) (js/parts.js:1821-1832; js/parts3d.js:882-892; js/combat.js:351-375)
- Guns, rockets and missiles fire along partAxis(p), in the blueprint plane: on a car (forward +x, up +y) a gun at rot 0 points UP; rot 3 points it forward (js/combat.js:249, :268, :280, :294; js/vessel.js:103, :106)
- Sideview draw order: sideLayer (leg/fin/wheel 0, hull 1, surfaces 2, pods 3) after depth (js/parts.js:1724-1728)
- Paint: SKINS has 26 entries (olive, tan, plus camouflage formats: a two-tone camo string with pat, or camo {k:'blotch'|'splinter'|'digital', c, seed, n, r, px}); NO_SKIN shapes (incl. 'wheel', 'gear') can't be painted; cleanSkin and skinOf/ownSkin (def.look) (js/parts.js:1491-1531)
- Design rows: designRows writes {type, x, y (3 dp), flip, rot}, plus z only off the default, att as an index and skin. partsFromDesign keeps unknown rows as null until att is resolved, clamps x/y to ±500, then runs cleanZ, cleanSkin and cleanAtt (js/parts.js:1226-1267)
- Design codes: designCode writes RSS1.<base64url {n,k,p:[[type,x,y,flip,rot]]}>, or RSS2. when rows carry [z,att,skin]. parseCode accepts only RSS1/RSS2, caps at 20000 rows, and turns an unknown kind into 'rocket' (js/builder.js:1490-1518)
- loadDesign: an unknown kind falls back to 'rocket'; the tab per kind is hard-coded twice ({rocket:'command', plane:'cabins', car:'ground', heli:'airEng'}) in loadDesign and in setKind (js/builder.js:359-376, :1216-1227)
- Car symmetry: mirrorOf for car/heli = (−x, y, z, mirrorRot): front and back, not left and right. Planes mirror z; rockets turn half round the stack. The symmetry toggle is stored in rss.symmetry, shared by rocket, car and heli (js/builder.js:385-400, :1230-1238)
- Placement: snap (2D) tries stacking, sides and corners, then snug slides the part along x onto the host's shape; pods and surfaces take the zFit path and need a surface host (falling back to touch); attach (3D) works from the hit normal; addPart checks Career.unlocked, inherits skinOf(host) and makes the symmetry twin (js/builder.js:629-777)
- quickAdd places every part at rot 0. Pods only try wings; side/leg/fin/rcs/girder parts go beside centred parts; control, chute and aero parts go on top, anything else at the bottom (js/builder.js:1167-1214)
- Tray: buildPalette shows the kind's cats, then More/Fewer; partFamilies groups look-alike jet parts by key regex /^(ru|us4|us5)/ and name /^(FS|MyG|Sugoi)-\d+/; specLine shows wing area, thrust, fuel, else mass (js/builder.js:1086-1127, :308-317)
- statsData (in changed()) = {mass, thrust, twr, dv, perStage, stageTwr, loose, cost (attached parts only), locked, fuel, air{stall, stallFlaps, wingArea, length, g, vne, mmax}}. It has no ground figures (no top speed, power/weight or armour) (js/builder.js:413-461)
- renderStats for car: hides the T/W row, the Δv label reads 'Fuel', and Stages still shows. The only car warnings are 'Add a Driver Cab…' (no control part) and 'Add car wheels under the chassis to drive.'; the generic ones (thrust off-centre, weight on one side, past a wing tip, loose parts, MP.designProblems, locked parts, funds) apply as well (js/builder.js:1260-1337 (car: 1269, 1283, 1301-1302))
- Launch: the button is disabled without a control part; App.launch checks locked parts and funds, then Flight.start(Builder.serialize()); the car tip is 'Drive with W/S and steer with A/D' (js/builder.js:1335, :1769, :1784; js/main.js:270-293)
- Kind buttons: four data-kind radio buttons in #kind-select; CSS grid repeat(4,1fr) (index.html:133-138; css/style.css:544)
- TEMPLATES (35): format {name, kind, tier, blurb, military?, parts:[[type,x,y,rot?,{z,skin,att}?]], paint?:{type:skin}}; the only car is 'buggy' (chassis, cab, 2 carWheel); templateRows sets flip = x<0 (js/builder.js:5-302, buggy :136-139)
- Hangar groups: {rocket, plane, military:'Military aircraft', car:'Cars', heli}. Any template with military:true goes under Military aircraft whatever its kind (js/builder.js:1615-1634)
- Battle money: Builder.mp = {budget: MP.budgetFor(spawn) (settings.budget, default $75k, x1.3 on a carrier), limits, kinds: MP_KINDS_PVP ['plane','heli','car']}. renderKind disables the other kinds. designProblems checks MP_MAX_PARTS 600, the budget, the kind and carrier size. battleDesign falls back to mustang/camel/skylark (js/mp.js:14-18, :428-476, :518-527; js/builder.js:1239-1244, :1314)
- Car-specific code outside the builder: drive frame F=+x, U=+y (so R=+z is the right side); steering = front wheels >0.3 m ahead of the COM; top speed = min wheel.speed of driving wheels, accel = max wheel.accel, independent of mass; combat counts kind 'car' as a ground target for AGMs; sandbox MP spawns cars on the runway (js/vessel.js:100-106, :972-1050; js/combat.js:459-462; js/mp.js:575)
- Tests that pin this area: fingerprint.js and traces.js iterate EVERY template (a new template shows up as 'new (not in the baseline)' until it is re-baselined); core.test.js checks that the jet templates don't clash and are fully joined; builder-shapes.mjs checks the tabs per kind (More shows 15 tabs = 14 categories + Fewer), the Weapons tab contents and every 3D model within its clash shape (test/fingerprint.js:8-33; test/traces.js:8; test/core.test.js:29-32; test/browser/builder-shapes.mjs:20-41, :160)

## Constraints
- Never change the key, w/h, twinZ or zLocked status of an existing part (cab, chassis, chassisL, carWheel, wheel, gun, cannon, gatling, turret, turretL, every pod...): saves store absolute centres, omit default depth and name parts by key. Add new parts instead.
- Do not change the behaviour fields of existing parts either (carWheel's wheel.speed/accel/track, a weapon's numbers, armor): fingerprint, traces and combat baselines pin them and old designs would drive or fight differently.
- New def fields must be opt-in, with readers falling back to the old behaviour when absent (as with fill||1 and glowAft||2).
- Keep per-part configuration in defs, not in saved rows. Rows are {type,x,y,flip,rot,z?,att?,skin?}; a new saved field would touch designRows, partsFromDesign, designCode (new prefix), parseCode, mpSpawnRows, Vessel.restore and NET_VERSION.
- Sanitise everything loaded: hasOwn(PARTS, type), cleanZ, cleanSkin, cleanAtt, plus mpSpawnRows for peers. Never put peer text into innerHTML.
- The legacy-twin rules (legacyTwin, pylonLink, the clash exemption, guessHost) must stay as they are.
- 2D = 3D: an outline in partPath0/HULLS is also the clash shape and, for lathe/extrude recipes, the 3D silhouette. builder-shapes.mjs fails if a model's vertices stand more than 1.5 cm outside its clash shape (fix with CLASH_PAD or CLASH_BOXED).
- partsClash and shapesClash decide placement only; never use them for joins (buildAdjacency).
- Draw code goes through drawPart (paint) and the skin-aware helpers, honouring PartPaint.flat.
- Builder edits must end in changed() (or changed(true) for live previews) and call Builder.redraw() when the picture changes without input (redraw.mjs guards this).
- Player-visible names (parts, paints, templates, blurbs, weapon labels) must not use real designations or nicknames (no Abrams, T-72, Leopard, Gepard, Tunguska, Pantsir, Bradley, BMP, Javelin, TOW, Kornet, MLRS, Patriot, S-400...); use altered names.
- Phone portrait: the kind selector is a 4-column grid (a fifth kind needs a CSS change); the tray tab strip scrolls with More sticky at the end; part names wrap to two lines; the stats panel folds.
- Multiplayer: AI and range targets stay off in rooms; damage to others goes through MP.sendHit(s); bump NET_VERSION if an older peer would build, pose or hit a ground craft differently; peer spawn rows are capped at MP_MAX_PARTS 600.
- Tests: new templates must be re-baselined in fingerprint and traces (confirm only new keys appear). The More-tab count (15) in builder-shapes.mjs moves with any new category. Keep README counts (257 parts, fourteen categories, 35 templates), the sw.js CACHE bump and the three controls lists in sync.
- Battery: buildAdjacency is O(n²) and runs only in changed(); keep per-drag work to groupFits/partsClash; any new per-frame builder work must earn its cost.

## Risks
- Driving ignores mass and power: top speed is the minimum wheel.speed of the driving wheels and acceleration the maximum wheel.accel (vessel.js:1040-1047). A 40 t tank on carWheels would be as quick as the buggy, so a tank update needs an engine/power model, which must be opt-in to keep the buggy's traces.
- Steering only turns the 'front' wheels (>0.3 m ahead of the COM); there is no skid steering, and a tracked vehicle with all contacts along its length steers oddly until the vessel learns tracks.
- partDepth gives every wheel 0.4 m of depth whatever its track (2 m on carWheel), so touch and clash ignore an axle's real width: two axles can be placed overlapping across z, and side armour skirts would not clash with the wheels they cover.
- Car symmetry is front/back: with symmetry on, an off-centre turret or gun placed fore or aft gets an automatic twin at −x, which surprises tank builders. Switching cars to left/right symmetry changes how existing car designs edit (not how they load).
- quickAdd places every part at rot 0: on a car, guns point up (+y) and turret bases face forward, so tapped weapons come out wrong unless quickAdd learns the car's orientation (rot 3).
- Turrets are AI-only, air-only (grounded = contactTime > 0 parks them), so on a tank they do nothing today; reusing kind 'turret' for player aim would change the existing turretL/turret behaviour unless gated by a new field.
- Combat treats kind 'car' as a ground target (AGMs lock it, AAMs can't); a new kind would lose that unless combat.js:459-462 learns it, while keeping 'car' brings tanks this behaviour for free.
- A template with military:true lands under 'Military aircraft' in the hangar; the battle fallback (battleDesign) only knows mustang/camel/skylark, so a ground-only mode would hand players a plane.
- Adding a new kind: older game versions and peers turn an unknown kind into 'rocket' (parseCode, loadDesign, flight.js:42), and the category maps are duplicated (builder.js:366, :1220), easy to miss one.
- def.armor only scales damage; War-Thunder-like penetration, angling and crew knock-outs need new combat readers (crew is unused today).
- Adding parts or categories breaks the pinned counts: README prose, the builder-shapes More-tab count, handbook §1.2, and the R&D lists per tier (main.js:337).
- Pods (twinZ + pod) placed on a car have no wing to hang on: snap falls back to side touch and quickAdd fails until it reaches its last-resort snap, so launcher boxes should probably not be `pod` parts.
- The partFamilies regexes are tied to jet names; a family of look-alike tanks would not group in the tray without changing them.

## Open questions
- Extend kind 'car' (fewest touchpoints, keeps ground-target treatment and PvP permission) or add a new kind such as 'tank' / 'ground' (its own symmetry, tabs, stats and spawn, but about 15 code sites to teach and a compat fallback to 'rocket' on old versions)?
- Should ground vehicles mirror left/right (z) like planes rather than front/back, and should existing car designs get that change too (only editing changes; saves load the same)?
- Tracks: one part per side (a twinZ pair like skids) or one axle-like part spanning both sides with `track`? And should the clash depth of wheels and tracks finally reflect their real width (only for new parts)?
- Turrets: a self-contained turret part with a built-in gun (like turretL), or a turret ring whose att-children (guns, launchers, radars) rotate with it in flight (needs vessel/render support for moving child parts)?
- Where do aim limits live (traverse rate, elevation and depression, stabiliser) — new weapon/def fields on the turret part?
- Engine/power model for ground vehicles: a new engine part with hp that sets top speed and acceleration from power/weight, with carWheel's fixed speed/accel kept as the fallback so the buggy is unchanged?
- Is the new multiplayer mode ground-only (Builder.mp.kinds limited to the ground kind, its own budget and stock fallback) or combined arms with planes and helicopters?
- Do ground weapons need new categories (e.g. 'Hulls', 'Turrets', 'Tracks', 'Ground weapons') in VEHICLE_KINDS.car.cats, or should they stay in Ground/Weapons with tray family groups?
- How should stats present a ground vehicle: top speed, hp/t, armour %, turret arcs, gun depression, total ammo — and which warnings should block Launch versus only warn (the owner prefers warnings)?
- Look-alike military vehicles as stock templates (altered names such as an 'M1'-like tank or a 'Gepard'-like SPAA): which ones, and should they come with their own camo skins?
