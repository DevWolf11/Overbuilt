# Multiplayer (js/mp.js, js/mpui.js, js/net.js): room modes, battle flow, spawns, design checks, ghost sync, hits, missiles, locks, chat, lobby UI, NET_VERSION; and how a new ground game mode plugs in

Multiplayer is a star network: the host's browser is the room (net.js). Clients send only to the host. The host stamps the real sender's pid on every message (`msg.f`) and relays it. Host-only types (`state`, `closed`, `ping`, and so on) are never relayed from clients. `MP.state` is the session, and the host owns it. Each change goes out as the whole object (`push`). Clients deep-copy it, and `applyState` runs `onPhase` when the phase or round changes. Clients edit only their own roster row, through `set {k,v}`, which the host checks per key.

There are two modes:
- **`sandbox`:** phase `free`, respawn allowed, everyone hostile.
- **`pvp`:** lobby → build → [test 60 s → polish] → battle → results. The timers live in `s.ends` (host clock) and are advanced by a 250 ms host `tick()`. Each player has one life (`alive`). `checkWin` ends the battle 3.5 s after one side is left. On time, the most players still flying wins, then the most kills.

Spawns come from the static `MP_SPAWNS` list: airfield bases and carriers, filtered by team and the carriers setting. Players are placed by slot through `placement`, `airfieldPlacement` and `carrierPlacement`. Budget and carrier size limits are checked only on the owner's machine (`designProblems`). A failing design is swapped for a stock plane (`battleDesign`).

Each player flies their own craft and broadcasts:
- `spawn` rows, cleaned on receipt by `mpSpawnRows`;
- 15 Hz snapshots `s`;
- 5 Hz part states `pv` (deflect, firing, ab, retracted, sweep, aimYaw, aimEl, ammo, deployed, used, gunOn), only for wing, thrust, weapon, chute and retractable-wheel parts;
- 2 Hz `hp`, plus `parts` when the part count changes.

Others show the craft as a `remote` Vessel ghost. It is dead-reckoned and never stepped by physics.

The shooter decides hits against its own ghost and sends `hit` to the victim, who applies the damage and reports its own `down`. The host books kills, deaths and the feed. Missiles, rockets and bombs (`shot`), guns, countermeasures, booms and radar `lock`s are replicated for show and for warnings.

The UI (mpui.js) covers the connect and lobby screens, the setup segments, the phase bar, the spawn map, chat, the kill feed, the scoreboard, results and the 3D-only HUD (name tags and an 8 km scope on screens at least 761 px wide).

`NET_VERSION` (6) must match exactly. Bump it whenever an older peer would build, pose or hit a different craft.

## Key facts
- Formats: 1v1 (n 2), 2v2 (4), 3v3 (6), ffa (teams 0, n 6). Defaults: format 1v1, build 180, test true, polish 120, budget 75000, carriers true, ff false, tod 'day', limit 900. Polish has no UI control, and the test phase is a fixed 60 s (mp.js:374). (js/mp.js:13-14)
- MP_CARRIER: bonus ×1.3, span 13 m, length 16 m, mass 12000 kg. MP_KINDS_PVP = ['plane','heli','car']. Rates: snapshot 15 Hz, pv 200 ms, hp 500 ms. MP_MAX_PARTS = 600. Team names are Faroes and Shetland. (js/mp.js:15-19)
- MP_SPAWNS has six static entries: three bases (faroes team 0, shetland team 1, scotland team -1 for FFA only) and three carriers (carrierA/B/C, teams 0/1/-1). Each has kind 'base' or 'carrier', with an airfield or carrier key into EARTH.airfields and EARTH.carriers (space.js:88-99). (js/mp.js:21-28)
- mpSpawnRows cleans a peer's spawn: the whole spawn is rejected if it is not an array or has more than 600 rows. A row is dropped if its id is not a positive safe integer, repeats an id, names a type that is not an own key of PARTS, or has non-finite x/y. x/y are clamped to ±500, rot is &3, flip becomes 0/1, z goes through cleanZ and skin through cleanSkin. att is kept only if it names an id in the same spawn, then cleanAtt breaks loops. Output rows are [id,type,x,y,rot,flip,z,att,skin]. (js/mp.js:36-50)
- hostile(a,b): anyone in the sandbox or FFA; in team battles only enemies, or teammates when ff is on. enemy(a,b), used for locks, tags and seekers: never a teammate. (js/mp.js:74-79)
- host() caps the room at 5 players for sandbox and 6 for pvp. The initial state is {mode, phase 'free' or 'lobby', ends, endsLen, round, result, settings:{...MP_DEFAULTS}, players, feed, t0, t0At}. The lobby text also hard-codes these caps: `${players.length}/${pvp ? 6 : 5}`. (js/mp.js:83-95; js/mpui.js:185)
- Player row: {pid,name,team,ready,spawn,alive,kills,deaths,dmg,ping,kind,flying}. In PvP the team is the pid in FFA, else the smaller team. p.kind is set through setMine('kind') but nothing reads it. (js/mp.js:204-213, 232, 547)
- applySet is the host's per-key check of client edits: ready; spawn (must be in spawnsFor); kind (≤8 chars); flying; team (lobby only, 0/1, not FFA); name (≤18); dmg (0..20000 per report, counted only in battle or sandbox). In build or polish it then runs maybeAllReady, and it always pushes. (js/mp.js:227-240)
- setSetting is host-only and lobby-only. A format change re-deals teams (FFA: pid; else i%2). Any other key is stored as given (`s.settings[k] = v`) without checking the value. (js/mp.js:246-256)
- push() sends the whole state to everyone and applies it locally. On clients, applyState deep-copies it with JSON, drops ghosts of missing players, shows new feed entries, runs onPhase on a phase or round change, then calls MPUI.refresh(). refresh rebuilds the lobby DOM (renderPlayers, renderSettings, renderFoot) on every push, even in flight. (js/mp.js:258-288; js/mpui.js:145-155)
- onPhase locks the room (Net.lock) in every pvp phase except lobby, so nobody can join a match in progress. Per phase: lobby → leaveFlight and the MP screen; build → pick a spawn, enterBuild(true); test → startFlight(true) (solo); polish → enterBuild(false); battle → startFlight(false); results → MPUI.results. (js/mp.js:289-323)
- startMatch resets players and the feed, then setPhase('build'). setPhase sets ends = hostNow + s·1000. For battle it also sets everyone alive and not flying, battleAt, tod and sides (the teams present). (js/mp.js:326-345)
- maybeAllReady: once everyone is ready, ends becomes 3 s from now; it is restored from endsFull if someone un-readies. (js/mp.js:347-353)
- tick() runs every 250 ms on a setInterval started in begin(). On the host it calls advance() when ends passes, and pushes pings every 4 s in lobby or free only. Clients report damage dealt as `set dmg` about once a second. (js/mp.js:115, 355-370)
- advance(): build → test (60 s) if settings.test, else battle(limit); test → polish; polish → battle; battle → finish(null,'time'). MP.advance() is the console handle that skips a phase. (js/mp.js:371-379)
- checkWin ends the battle 3.5 s (setTimeout plus a `finishing` flag) after at most one team still has an alive player, unless only one side started. On time, finish() ranks teams by players alive, then kills; a tie is a draw. result = {winner (team, or pid in FFA), why, ffa, players copy}, then setPhase('results', 0). Results has no timer: the host presses Back to the lobby. (js/mp.js:381-408)
- spawnsFor filters out carriers unless settings.carriers, and keeps all spawns in FFA, only the player's team otherwise. defaultSpawn: in FFA the base the fewest players picked, else the team's base. budgetFor is settings.budget, ×1.3 at a carrier. limitsFor returns MP_CARRIER only at a carrier. (js/mp.js:411-433)
- designProblems: more than 600 parts in any mode. In PvP also: over budget for this.spawnId; kind not in MP_KINDS_PVP; at a carrier, not a plane or heli, span over 13, length over 16 or mass over 12 t (measure). These checks run only on the owner's machine, and peers never re-check. (js/mp.js:446-462)
- battleDesign takes the player's design, keeps only the part of the craft joined to the control part, checks it, and otherwise falls back to the first of the stock planes ['mustang','camel','skylark'] that passes, else TEMPLATES.camel, with a toast. The only car template is the unarmed Dune Buggy, and there are no ground stock vehicles. (js/mp.js:464-476; js/builder.js:136-139)
- launch() in PvP build or polish toggles ready; toggleReady serialises the design, then requires Builder.ctrl and no problems. enterBuild sets Builder.mp = {budget, limits, kinds: MP_KINDS_PVP}, forces 'plane' if the current kind isn't allowed, and opens the spawn map 400 ms later. Builder uses mp.kinds to disable kind buttons and MP.designProblems for its stat warnings. (js/mp.js:495-527; js/builder.js:1242, 1314)
- startFlight(solo): despawns any previous craft, picks battleDesign in PvP, then placement(spawnId, kind). The time is tod + (hostNow − battleAt)/1000 in PvP, else the sandbox's shared clock. Then Flight.start(design, {place, time, mp:{mode, solo, phase}}). In the shared world it then sends sendSpawn, setMine('flying', true) and, in PvP, kind. (js/mp.js:529-554)
- respawn() is sandbox only (returns in PvP). Flight's revert calls MP.respawn. The flight code enforces one craft and one life in PvP: switchTo and cycleVessel refuse when mp.mode==='pvp'. (js/mp.js:556-560; js/flight.js:113, 293, 1121)
- placement(): sandbox slots go 220 m down the runway, or ±35 m steps on the helipad. In PvP the slot is the player's index among players at the same spawn, sorted by pid; carrier → carrierPlacement; faroes → runway +230 m per slot, or helipad +40 m per slot; airfields → airfieldPlacement (planes and cars at the threshold, 230 m apart, a side offset after three; helis beside the strip). Flight.start puts the craft there with v.placeOnSurface(EARTH, siteAngle(P.e), launchRot, P.z, P.yaw). placeOnSurface samples the terrain height at that one point only. (js/mp.js:571-588, 941-957; js/flight.js:49-56; js/vessel.js:1069-1081)
- sendSpawn rows: [id, type, x, y, rot, flip, z (partZ), att (a host id or 0), skin]. The snapshot `s` carries th (host ms), b (body id), P and V (0.01 precision), Q (w,x,y,z), W, C (centre of mass x,y,z), g (gear up), l (landed). (js/mp.js:591-621)
- pv rows are sent only for parts with wing, thrust, weapon, chute or a retractable wheel: [id, deflect, firing, ab, retracted, sweep, aimYaw, aimEl, ammo, deployed, used, gunOn]. Only changed rows are sent, plus all of them every 2 s. Wheel spin and steering are not sent; ghosts skip physics, so their wheels never get p.spin (it is set only in Vessel.ground). (js/mp.js:622-639, 793-809; js/vessel.js:1049; js/flight.js:524)
- onLocalDestroyed: a test flight offers Fly again. In the shared world it sends `down {by, P}`; `by` is lastHitBy.remote if that hit came within 20 s. On the host it calls recordDown directly. In the sandbox it offers a hangar/fly-again modal; in PvP it starts spectating after 1.8 s. (js/mp.js:649-676)
- recordDown (host): requires p.flying, sets flying false (and alive false in battle), adds a death, gives the killer a kill and a feed line, runs checkWin, pushes. On the host, onSpawn sets p.flying = true when a spawn arrives. (js/mp.js:678-688, 704)
- buildGhost builds the parts with makePart, sets p.id to the owner's id, then a new Vessel with buildAdjacency. It sets v.remote = pid, kind (falls back to 'plane' unless it is in VEHICLE_KINDS), sas off, full ammo, hiddenUntilSnap. (js/mp.js:706-726)
- onSnap checks P, V, Q and W with mpNums, C with 3 or 2 values, th finite, b a known body, and |Q|>0.5. It stores P.snap whether or not we are flying (so the host always has every flying player's last position). With no design it asks for one with `want` (at most every 3 s). poseGhost dead-reckons up to 0.75 s, snaps when more than 60 m off, and accepts C only within reach+1 of com0. A ghost silent for 6 s freezes. (js/mp.js:744-792)
- Hits: sendHits goes only to a hostile ghost's owner, as `hit {to, list:[[partId, dmg]], k}`. The host adds the damage to its own score directly; clients batch it as dmgOut. onHit requires m.to === me and a hostile sender, takes at most 64 rows with damage capped at 5000, looks up parts in every local non-AI vessel, and sets lastHitBy first. (js/mp.js:845-874)
- Missiles: `msl {id,b,P,V,D,tg,radar,glide,agm}`, then `mp` updates at 10 Hz (syncMissiles), then `mx`. Receivers fly a dmg-0 replica (Combat.netMissile) and re-target it on every update when tg === me and the shooter is hostile. Rockets and bombs go as `shot`; netShot knows only bomb vs rocket. Guns send `gun {k: partType, on}`; turrets use gunOn from pv (netGuns). Flares and chaff go as `cm {P,V,chaff 0/1/2}`. (js/mp.js:887-916; js/combat.js:505-518, 823-873)
- Locks: sendLock sends `lock {on}` to the target only. MP.rwr maps pid → last report time and expires entries after 2.5 s in beforeUpdate. Combat re-sends a lock every 1 s or more while it holds. Only an RWR part shows it (Combat lockers). (js/mp.js:58, 200, 791, 917-921; js/combat.js:1173, 1317-1319)
- Chat goes to everyone and lands in MPUI.chat(m.f, m.text) through createTextNode (safe). Sends are capped at 140 characters, but neither the length nor the type of received text is checked. There is no team-only chat. (js/mp.js:182; js/mpui.js:109-114, 413-421)
- NET_VERSION = 6. The host rejects a hello with any other ver. HOST_ONLY = {~, hello, welcome, reject, ping, closed, join, leave, warn, state, result}; clients ignore these unless f===1. The host overwrites msg.f with the real sender and drops relays over 256 KB. (js/net.js:11, 39, 171, 182-194, 268)
- Lobby setup segments in renderSettings: Format 1v1/2v2/3v3/FFA; Money $45k/$75k/$120k; Build time 2/3/5 min; Test flight checkbox; Carriers checkbox (always shown); Friendly fire (not in FFA); Time of day Day/Dusk/Night; Time limit 10/15/25 min. Only the host can edit, and only in the lobby. start() asks before a solo start or uneven teams, and refuses an empty team. (js/mpui.js:123-142, 190-230)
- Phase bar: label, timer from s.ends − hostNow (urgent under 15 s, except in battle). Info line: cost / budget · spawn · ready while building; 'Faroes N · M Shetland' alive counts in battle. Ready and Spawn buttons in the builder only, plus a Score button. (js/mpui.js:251-292; index.html:302-310)
- Spawn map: fixed MAP bounds e −9000..31000, z −9000..21000. spawnAt switches on carrier, 'faroes' or airfield. Bases are drawn as circles, carriers as ship shapes. pickSpawn updates Builder.mp budget and limits. (js/mpui.js:296-410)
- Scoreboard columns: Pilot, Team, Kills, Lost, Damage, Ping. Results modal: Victory/Defeat/Draw with the table, locked (MP.modalLocked); the host gets Back to the lobby. (js/mpui.js:447-483)
- MPUI.drawHUD runs only on the 3D path (main.js:102); the 2D fallback has no multiplayer HUD. It draws diamond tags with name and distance for spotted ghosts (spotted: friends always, enemies within 6 km×(1−0.5·stealth), or on our own radar within 60° of the nose), the radar scope (drawRadar, only at W ≥ 761, bases cached once in this.bases) and the spectate label. (js/mpui.js:495-590; js/main.js:101-102)
- Seekers' view of ghosts: air seekers take enemy ghosts that are !landed && kind !== 'car'. Ground seekers take landed ghosts or kind 'car'. turretStep tracks only air candidates and parks the guns whenever the craft touches the ground. (js/combat.js:454-465, 355-375)
- Planets do not rotate. Ground position from a body-centred P: east = −wrapAngle(atan2(y,x) − padAngle)·R and z = asin(z/r)·R. drawRadar already turns (e,z) into world coordinates: r·cos(ph)·cos(a), etc. (js/flight.js:847; js/mpui.js:565-569; js/space.js:101)
- Silly flags are off in any PvP room; Combat mode is forced off in any room (no AI, targets or SAMs); time warp is off in every room. (js/util.js:143; js/combat.js:27; js/flight.js:202, 512)

## Constraints
- Sandbox and the existing air battles must play exactly as before. New settings must default so that an air battle is unchanged: MP_DEFAULTS gets game:'air', and checkWin and finish keep the last-team-flying rule for air.
- Bump NET_VERSION (net.js:11) for any new part, paint or vehicle kind, any spawn row, snapshot or pv layout change, any new message meaning, or any change to what a part does on its owner's side that decides hits. The host rejects any other version at hello (net.js:171).
- Peers are untrusted. Check every new field with mpNums, mpBody, clamps and Map lookups. Route new peer data through mpSpawnRows-style cleaning. Never put peer text into innerHTML (UI.modal renders a string body as HTML). New host-only types go into HOST_ONLY and their handlers check m.f===1.
- Damage to another player's craft must go through MP.sendHit or sendHits, where the shooter decides and the victim applies. Combat.damage stays a no-op on v.remote. Never renumber the parts of a flying craft: hit, pv, hp and parts messages all use the owner's part ids.
- Shared deadlines (capture timers, respawn timers, s.ends, battleAt) use Net.hostNow(); send rates use performance.now(). Simulation time is per peer and time warp is forced off.
- Objective state (tickets, capture ownership and progress, respawn eligibility, match result) must be decided and stored by the host in MP.state or a host-only message, never by clients. Clients may only edit their own row through applySet keys the host checks.
- Combat.reset must run on every new flight (respawns included); startFlight → Flight.start → resetState already does this. AI, range targets, SAMs and auto countermeasures stay off in any room (mpOn).
- Phone portrait layout: #mp-bar moves under the top bar and the radar scope is hidden under 761 px (css/style.css:902-913; mpui.js:542). Any new HUD must fit both. The lobby grid collapses to one column under 860 px.
- Battery: push() re-sends the whole state and every peer rebuilds the lobby DOM in MPUI.refresh. Don't push at objective-tick rates; use a small dedicated message, and redraw only what changed.
- No modules and no build step. Top-level names are global across files, so check for duplicate const or function names. A new js file needs a script tag in index.html, an entry in sw.js SHELL, a CACHE bump and a README layout line.
- Spawns go through Flight.start's placement {e, z, yaw[, catapult, carrier, soft]} and Vessel.placeOnSurface, which samples the terrain at a single point, so ground spawns need flat ground (EARTH.flats or flattened airfields).
- Keep the controls in sync in the README table, App.showHelp and Flight.controlsHelp; the MP help line is at flight.js:499.

## Risks
- Respawning in PvP breaks assumptions throughout the code. alive=false is final (recordDown); checkWin ends the battle when one side has nobody alive; onLocalDestroyed goes straight to spectating; respawn() returns in PvP; Flight blocks switchTo and cycleVessel in PvP; the results flow keys on phase changes. Each needs a per-game branch, or a ground match will end at the first death or leave players unable to come back.
- Capture presence would rest on positions each client reports about itself (P.snap), like everything else in the trust model. A modified client can sit in a zone it isn't in. This is acceptable for a host-relayed P2P game, but worth knowing.
- The host is a player. Its tick is a 250 ms setInterval, which browsers throttle in hidden tabs (about 1/s, and much less after minutes hidden), and a phone host stops entirely with its screen off. Capture progress and tickets would stall or jump. The wake lock (MP.awake) helps only while the game is on screen.
- Full-state push() costs. Every push re-renders the lobby DOM on every peer (mpui.js:153), and battles already push about once a second per shooting client (`set dmg`). Objective updates through push() would add CPU and battery cost.
- Ghost fidelity for ground vehicles. Ghost wheels and tracks don't spin or steer (no spin or steer in pv; ghosts skip physics). A turret ring that is not a weapon part wouldn't sync (pv filter at mp.js:629). Tank shells would replicate as rockets (netShot). Hit boxes rely on the bound on the C (centre of mass) field.
- Seeker and turret rules are air-centric. A ghost of a new ground kind that bounces off the ground (l=0 in the snapshot) becomes lockable by air seekers unless the filter at combat.js:458 treats the new kind like 'car'. Existing turrets park on the ground (combat.js:356-359), so they do nothing on tanks.
- Spotting is very generous for ground combat: any enemy within 6 km is tagged and shown on the scope, regardless of terrain (mpui.js:529-539). That reveals tanks behind hills and removes the War Thunder spotting game.
- Stock fallback: battleDesign's final `|| TEMPLATES.camel` (mp.js:473) would drop a plane into a ground battle, and no ground stock vehicles exist.
- Room size: maxPlayers is 6 and the lobby hard-codes '/6' and '/5' (mpui.js:185). Bigger ground teams mean N² relay traffic through the host on a phone.
- The room is locked for the whole PvP match (Net.lock at mp.js:291), so nobody can join a ground battle in progress. Changing that touches accept() and addPlayer mid-battle.
- Existing loose ends a new mode would inherit: onDown reads m.P unchecked (mp.js:834); received chat text has no length or type check (mp.js:182 → mpui.js:416); setSetting stores values unchecked (host only); p.kind is stored but never used.
- The 2D fallback view has no multiplayer HUD at all (main.js:102 runs only on the 3D path), so capture markers would be 3D only unless they are added to Flight.render too.

## Open questions
- Should the ground game be a setting inside state.mode 'pvp' (keeps the MP.pvp semantics, fewer touch points) or a new state.mode such as 'ground' (cleaner separation, but 30-plus 'pvp' checks to revisit)?
- What is the respawn model: one life (as today), tickets with free respawns, or a War Thunder-style lineup with spawn-point costs? Each changes alive, checkWin and finish, onLocalDestroyed and battleDesign differently.
- Where is the ground battlefield? Candidates: the Faroes space centre and runway, the causeway to Shetland (space.js:76), the Shetland and Scotland airfields. Does it need new flattened zones or capture-point data in space.js?
- Combined arms: can planes and helicopters join a ground battle (as in War Thunder), and with what spawns and costs? Should air battles allow ground vehicles beyond 'car'?
- Should capture presence be judged by the host from snapshots (simple, trusts self-reported positions), or by each client reporting 'I am in zone X' (also trust-based, fewer computations on the host)?
- Should spotting for ground vehicles use terrain line of sight (Terrain height sampling along the ray), a shorter range, or War Thunder-style spotting reported by teammates?
- Will team sizes grow beyond 6 (4v4, 5v5)? That needs bandwidth measurements on a phone host.
- Should players be able to join a ground battle in progress, given tickets and respawns?
- Is a new vehicle kind (e.g. 'tank') coming, which means VEHICLE_KINDS, ghost kind fallbacks, seeker filters and a NET_VERSION bump, or will ground vehicles stay kind 'car'?
