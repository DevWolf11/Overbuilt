# Critic: what the maps missed

- `landed` means "touching the ground and slower than 1.5 m/s", not "on the ground". Every system that reads `!landed` as "airborne" therefore treats a driving vehicle as flying. @ js/vessel.js:1060 (landed = contactTime>0.2 && speed<1.5). Readers: combat.js:1121 (`flying` gate for AA, SAM and frigate), combat.js:1145 (fighter spawn), combat.js:1032 (fighter tgtOk), combat.js:458-459 (air-seeker candidates), combat.js:462, mp.js:620 (snapshot `l`), flight.js:527 (stepping and rails), flight.js:1322 (HUD 'landed' label), flight.js:1332 (Mach row): Measured on the Dune Buggy at the runway, at 1/120 s: once it moves past 1.5 m/s, landed=false and altitude()=41.8 m. altitude() is height above sea level and the runway sits at 40 m, so the `flying` gate is true at every speed above a walk. A new ground kind would be an air-seeker target whenever it drives, and so would its ghost. Only the literal `kind === 'car'` keeps cars out today.
- In solo Hostile mode, moving cars are already engaged. AA guns within 3.5 km fire (aaFire has no line-of-sight check; only the terrain end-point test stops rounds). SAM sites fire when altitude is over 150 m above sea level, which the Highlands at 250 m reach. Up to two AI fighters spawn every 45 s as soon as the player is not 'landed', then chase and shoot (they pull up below 450 m above ground). @ js/combat.js:1121-1146, 1026-1064, 962-972: The ground update must decide this explicitly. No test pins this behaviour, but changing the gates changes how existing cars play in Hostile mode. Default targets mode is 'practice' (world.js:7), so most players haven't seen it.
- There is no collision between vessels anywhere. Ground contact probes only Terrain and carrier decks. Tanks pass through each other, through other players' ghosts, wrecks and debris, and through range targets, which are spheres for shots only. Buildings and trees are visual. @ js/vessel.js:930-1064 (ground: Terrain.surfaceAt/deckAt only); grep finds no craft-vs-craft test in vessel.js, flight.js, combat.js or mp.js: Ramming, blocking, using wrecks as cover and pushing are all missing. Spawns can overlap: in sandbox, MP.placement returns null for any kind other than plane, car or heli (mp.js:572-577), so every player's vehicle of a new kind would spawn at the same point.
- Blasts hurt their own shooter. explode() damages every non-remote vessel in radius, including the owner. The 1.5 s owner skip covers direct segment hits only. A ground-launched missile gets no launch speed and flies unpowered and unguided for 0.35 s. @ js/combat.js:127-146 (explode), 552 (owner skip), 280-281 (launchMissile: `down` is zero for non-planes, velocity = vehicle velocity), 529-530 (no motor or guidance before 0.35 s): Measured: an Attack Missile on a parked buggy's cab roof, muzzle 3.4 m above the runway, fired with no lock. It dropped 0.6 m in the coast, then flew along its axis still sinking, and hit the runway about 0.83 s after launch, roughly 35 m ahead (its blast radius is 20 m). A lower ATGM or rocket rack, a close HE hit, or a missile with no lock will strike the ground and can self-damage. Ground launchers need a launch impulse or loft.
- Wrecks differ between the owner and everyone else. Locally, losing the cab leaves the rest of the vehicle as a controlless hulk that stays drawn and stepped and keeps smoking or burning from its damaged parts. On every other screen the ghost is dropped on 'down' and the owner stops sending snapshots, so the wreck vanishes. Debris pieces are never replicated. @ js/flight.js:372-389 (breakParts keeps v), js/vessel.js:1121 (splitComponents keeps the parts[0] component when no control is left), js/combat.js:1175-1189 (smoke over all vessels within 5 km), js/mp.js:661, 830-837 (down → dropGhost), mp.js:611 (no snapshots once destroyed), mp.js:810-816 (onParts only removes parts): Knocked-out tanks as cover and burning hulks (War Thunder feel) need a replicated wreck state, and a NET_VERSION bump.
- Only the active craft runs turrets, and AI craft fire only fixed 'gun' parts. Vessel-based AI (the spawnFighter pattern) is wired as fighters throughout. @ js/combat.js:491-495 (turretStep only for F.active), 490 (AI: weapon.kind 'gun' only), 1148-1151 (every o.ai vessel is flown by flyFighter, and killed when not hostile or not near, so practice mode kills it), 1144-1145 (o.ai counts toward the 2-fighter cap), 458 (moving o.ai vessels are air candidates), 461-462 (the ground branch never takes local vessels), flight.js:146 (serialize drops v.ai): AI tanks, SPAAs or SAMs built as real Vessels can't just set v.ai. They need their own AI branch, fire path, candidate classification and practice-mode lifetime. Ground seekers also can't lock any local non-ghost vessel.
- A vehicle survives as long as any control part remains, because v.control is computed fresh over all parts. That is already a crude crew model. @ js/vessel.js:150 (get control → findControlPart, parts.js:1287-1290), flight.js:385-387 (onDestroyed only when !v.control): Several cabs or crew parts give redundancy for free, which is a cheap way into a War Thunder-style crew knock-out. Note that the getter scans every part on each call, and it is called in hot paths.
- Which vessels get physics: remote ghosts never. Controlled vessels that are not 'landed' are stepped at any distance. Uncontrolled moving vessels more than 6 km away go on Kepler rails and are deleted if their path dips into the air. @ js/flight.js:520-536: Vessel-based AI convoys cost a ground() step wherever they are while moving. A driverless car or rolling wreck far away silently disappears.
- Switching craft leaves the previous craft's stick and brake as they were, so a car left while W was held keeps driving and never sleeps. @ js/flight.js:588-605 (readControls writes only the active vessel), 290-303 (switchTo resets nothing), 633 (only landed vessels sleep): This applies to any solo multi-vehicle play or split vehicles. It is cheap to fix by zeroing ctl and brake on a switch, but it is a behaviour change.
- Save and resume details that matter for new state @ js/vessel.js:1166-1199, js/flight.js:144-152, 583-584, js/main.js:300, js/combat.js:929-933: restore keeps `d.kind || 'rocket'` without checking VEHICLE_KINDS, unlike flight.js:42, builder.js:362/1516 and mp.js:539/701/717, so tables keyed by kind must tolerate unknown kinds. A throw in restore wipes the save (main.js:300). serialize lists per-part fields one by one: aimYaw/aimEl, cool, side, spin and any shell or reload state are not saved, and v.wsel resets. restore's Object.assign(p, q) copies every saved field unsanitised (hp, ammo and store already are). AI vessels, shots and range targets are not saved; targets respawn reseeded from F.time. Solo saves happen every 10 s.
- Sound has a single engine loop and is mostly active-craft only @ js/flight.js:555-558, js/audio.js:24-35, 74-79, 101-103, js/combat.js:76, 126, 260, 393, 579, js/mp.js:871: The engine loop is lowpass brown noise scaled by thrust/maxThrust of the active craft, so a vehicle with no thrust parts drives in silence. There is no tyre, track or gearbox sound. Sound.gun has two timbres and plays only for the active craft: other players' and AI fire is silent. Hit ticks play only on the active craft, explosions only within 6 km, and nothing is positional. A new loop costs battery while the context runs (Sound.settle, audio.js:41-60).
- Haptics are sparse @ js/flight.js:1478 (haptic), used at 275, 326, 381, 571, 613, 771, 795, 1087 (FIRE press, 10 ms); builder.js:1930: There is no vibration on being hit, on gun recoil or on reload. iOS Safari has no navigator.vibrate, so haptics can only add to, never replace, visual feedback.
- How the secret silly settings meet ground vehicles @ js/util.js:131-147 (Silly.on is off only when MP.pvp, so the flags apply in sandbox rooms), vessel.js:967-970, flight.js:596-601, world.js:58, util.js:149 with flight.js:608-622, main.js:147, flight.js:1456: bouncy: no impact breaks or dents, and 0.75 restitution on every contact with vn<-2, so new track or suspension contacts bounce unless gated. drunk: perturbs ctl.v/h/r, so it reaches a new aim input only if aim goes through ctl. hollywood: explosion effects ×3, damage unchanged. boomy: soundBarrier runs for every kind, so any car or tank past 25 m/s gets the boom and the 'Allegedly' toast. retro2d: forces the 2D flight view, which has no Combat or MP HUD. ghostCam: freezes the camera after a crash.
- Career gating and missions @ js/parts.js:825-831 (TIERS 0-4, rocket-themed names), career.js:11, 47, flight.js:857-860, main.js:271-275, 332-337, builder.js:1622, parts.js:354/369/398 (cannon, agm, turretL tier 2): Parts must have tier ≤ 4 or they never unlock. Every armed part is tier 2+, so a fresh career can't build an armed vehicle until $80k of R&D. The R&D modal lists every part name per tier. The only ground mission is drive1k and it counts kind 'car' only. Launch messages say 'rocket' ('This rocket uses parts…', 'this rocket costs').
- The hangar lists templates by looping a fixed groups object, so templates of a new kind are never shown. Saved designs of an unknown kind show as 'Rocket'. @ js/builder.js:1615-1619 (groups {rocket, plane, military, car, heli}; military:true → 'Military aircraft'), 1584-1588, parts.js:2704-2724 (thumbnails: 2D side view, no turret aim), index.html:134-137 and ui.js:80 (four hard-coded kind buttons and their icons), builder.js:1239-1243: A 'tank' kind, or ground templates marked military, would vanish or be filed under aircraft. A new kind also needs a kind button and icon.
- The builder never warns that one wing part turns driving off @ js/vessel.js:245 (aircraft = wings.length>0), 975 (canDrive = !aircraft && …), 517-518 and 557 (the stick then rotates the craft as a plane), builder.js:1301-1302 (the only car warning is about wheels): A spoiler, or a launcher rail built from a wing part, silently makes a vehicle undrivable. Shooting the wing off makes it drive again.
- The existing PvP bases are on different islands @ js/mp.js:21-23 (faroes team 0 at the runway, shetland team 1), space.js:76 (causeway 3300-17200 m, ashore 17850), terrain.js trackHalf (14 m on the causeway), mp.js:14 (limit 900 s): By road, from the runway at e 760 to the Shetland strip (e 18.6 km) is about 18 km. The buggy needs about 8 min at 38 m/s; a 15 m/s tank about 20 min, past the default 15-minute limit, on a single causeway. The Scotland FFA base is across the sea, and the sea is solid ground (driveable at about 6.7 m/s). A ground mode needs its own spawns.
- Shared UI text assumes aircraft or rockets @ js/mp.js:82 ('Pilot'), 474, 551 ('Last team flying wins'), 672, 839 ('shot down!'), mpui.js:195, 456 (Pilot column), index.html:50 ('How to fly'), 84, 130 ('Rocket stats'), main.js:271 ('Add a Command Pod or Probe Core first'), flight.js:39 (default name 'Rocket'), 1416 ('No engines running'): Ground battles would read 'shot down' and 'last team flying'. These strings need per-mode or kind-neutral wording.
- The combat HUD is often absent @ js/combat.js:1205 (hidden while F.destroyed, so also while spectating), main.js:93-103 (3D path only), main.js:146-152 (use3D is false until three.js is lazy-loaded, and always with retro2d): A gunner sight or reload ring drawn in Combat.drawHUD won't show in the first frames on a slow phone, in 2D, in the map or globe, or while spectating.
- Cars use front/back symmetry, so a forward gun's twin points backwards @ js/builder.js:385-391 (mirror x→−x with mirrorRot), parts.js:866 (mirrorRot 3→1): With symmetry on (the default for 'rss.symmetry'), a rot 3 forward gun placed fore or aft of the centre line gets a rot 1 twin firing to the rear. This goes beyond the 'surprise twin' the parts map noted.

## Wrong claims
- COMBAT map summary and risk: 'Only the AA guns, SAM sites and the frigate shoot back, and only at an airborne player'; 'A ground vehicle with weapons is invisible to AI AA, SAMs and fighters (the flying gate at combat.js:1121; tgtOk at 1032)'. -> `flying` is !landed && altitude()>40, where landed requires speed<1.5 m/s (vessel.js:1060) and altitude is above sea level. A buggy driving on the 40 m runway measured landed=false and altitude 41.8 m, so the gate is true. In Hostile mode, AA within 3.5 km fires at it, fighters spawn on !v.landed (combat.js:1145), and tgtOk (!landed || speed>20) is true for any moving vehicle. SAMs need altitude above 150 m above sea level.
- TERRAIN map: 'Hostile AA/SAM/frigate engage only a flying player (!landed && altitude > 40; SAMs > 150 m), so ground vehicles are ignored today' (combat.js:1121-1139). -> The same error. A moving ground vehicle is 'not landed', and the altitude is measured above sea level, so any driving on ground above about 38 m (the runway, most of Shetland's high ground, North Scotland) draws AA fire. Ground above 150 m (the Highlands) draws SAMs.
- COMBAT map: 'flyFighter's tgtOk requires !pl.landed || pl.speed()>20, so AI ignores a parked ground vehicle.' -> True only for a vehicle at rest (under 1.5 m/s). Any vehicle moving faster than 1.5 m/s is 'not landed', so fighters engage it, and the fighter-spawn check at combat.js:1145 uses the same !v.landed.
- MULTIPLAYER map risk: 'A ghost of a new ground kind that bounces off the ground (l=0 in the snapshot) becomes lockable by air seekers unless the filter at combat.js:458 treats the new kind like car'. -> It doesn't need to bounce. The snapshot's l is v.landed (mp.js:620), which is 0 whenever the vehicle drives faster than 1.5 m/s. A new ground kind's ghost is an air-seeker candidate (combat.js:459) and not a ground-seeker one (combat.js:462) whenever it is moving.
- VEHICLE map risk: 'Cars use the rocket SAS bar. Pressing a direction mode (prograde etc.) pitches the car's roof toward it, fighting the wheels.' -> Measured on the Dune Buggy at about 36 m/s on the runway with hold, prograde, radialOut and retrograde: maximum tilt 0.0° and speed 36.6 vs 36.4 m/s. The torque is requested, but it is capped at the halved reaction-wheel authority (2,000 N·m), and the rigid wheel contacts overwhelm it. On the ground these modes do nothing visible (the FLIGHT map's 'dead UI' measurement agrees). They could only act in the air or on a light vehicle with large torque.
- RENDERING map risk: 'A destroyed vessel's VesselMesh is disposed the frame it dies or empties, and parts blown off just vanish with an explosion. There is no persistent burning-wreck model for a knocked-out tank (the range targets have one; players don't).' -> Locally, losing the cab does not make the vessel dead. The rest stays as a controlless hulk (flight.js:384-387): drawn, stepped, and smoking or burning from its damaged parts (combat.js:1175-1189). Only parts that explode (breakParts) vanish; parts that are only ripped off become tumbling debris vessels (flight.js:258-282). The wreck disappears only on other players' screens, because 'down' drops the ghost (mp.js:830-837).
