# Flight screen and controls (js/flight.js, js/main.js, index.html flight HUD, css/style.css; with the camera in render3d.js and the 2D fallback in world.js)

Flight.start (flight.js:28-64) builds a Vessel from the design rows and places it with placeOnSurface (vessel.js:1069-1081) at EARTH.sites[VEHICLE_KINDS[kind].site]. A car goes to the runway (760 m east, z 0, facing east, launchRot 0), or to a multiplayer placement (mp.js:571-586).

Each frame App.frame (main.js:81-106) calls Flight.update, which calls readControls (flight.js:588-606). That sums the held keys (stored by e.code) and the touch stick into v.ctl {v,h,r}, each -1..1. Shift/Ctrl move v.throttle at 0.9/s, B or the Brake button sets v.brake, and V or FIRE sets Flight.firing.

Flight maps no input by kind; Vessel decides what the inputs mean. On wheels, a car's ctl.v sets the target wheel speed and ctl.h turns the front wheels (vessel.js:973-1046). For a car on wheels, ctl.r and v.throttle do nothing, there is no skid steering, and the hull levels itself (vessel.js:534-544).

The camera is Render3D's follow cam (render3d.js:2381-2434). It orbits the craft's centre at a distance set by F.scale and the fixed 50° FOV, sitting behind the nose for cars. The player's drag offset (orbitBy) is added to a heading that eases after the hull. C or a double-click resets it, and the wheel or a pinch zooms by changing F.scale.

The HUD is DOM (#flight-ui, index.html:202-298), refreshed by updateHUD every 0.08 s. Canvas overlays are drawn every frame on top: World.drawOverlay3D, Combat.drawHUD (pipper, brackets, MSL markers, RWR) and MPUI.drawHUD, in 3D only (main.js:96-103). Touch controls are always shown, desktop included. The CSS has separate layouts for phones in portrait (≤760 px wide) and on their side (≤500 px tall).

There is no pointer lock, gamepad, gunner view or FOV change. Turrets aim themselves at aircraft (combat.js:355-374) and park on the ground. The 2D fallback is a side cut through the XY plane: it ignores z and draws no combat HUD.

## Key facts
- Placement: kind comes from design.kind (falls back to 'rocket'); without opts.place the craft is put at EARTH.sites[K.site].east, z 0, yaw 0 (a float plane with no wheels goes to the 'bay', facing π); a heli starts at throttle 0 (js/flight.js:42-52)
- VEHICLE_KINDS: car = {site:'runway', launchRot:0, cats:['ground','utility','structure','weapons']}; plane also uses the runway site; heli uses the helipad (js/parts.js:25-30)
- Sites: pad 0, helipad 160, runway 760 (planes and cars at the west end, facing east), bay -2300; the paved strip runs 700-2700; the causeway to Shetland runs 3300-17200, ashore at 17850 (js/space.js:69-76)
- Multiplayer placement for cars: sandbox puts each player on the runway at +220 m per slot (soft); the PvP 'faroes' spawn uses the runway at +230 m per slot; airfields and carriers go through airfieldPlacement/carrierPlacement. A catapult is only armed for planes (js/mp.js:571-586; js/flight.js:56)
- Start zoom: scale = clamp(min(0.42·innerHeight/tall, 0.5·innerWidth/wide), 1.5, 40), where a car's tall is its bounds height (planes are measured lying on their side) (js/flight.js:57-59)
- readControls: ctl.v = W/↑ − S/↓ + stick.v; ctl.h = D/→ − A/← + stick.h; ctl.r = E − Q + stick.r, each clamped to ±1. The 'drunk' silly flag adds drift. The throttle changes by ±0.9/s on Shift/PageUp and Ctrl/PageDown; brake = KeyB || touchBrake; firing = KeyV || touchFire (js/flight.js:588-606)
- readControls runs on every update, before the paused gate; Flight.update clamps dt to 0.05 (js/flight.js:505-511)
- keydown is ignored outside the flight scene, while a modal is open, or while typing in an INPUT/TEXTAREA. It calls preventDefault for Space, the arrows and Tab, and skips actions on e.repeat. Key 1-6 maps to the rocket autopilot for every kind but planes (planes get PLANE_SAS_KEYS 1-4), so cars get the rocket set (js/flight.js:980-988)
- Flight key map: Space stage, M map, N globe/system, , . warp, Z/X full/cut, [ ] switch vessel, Tab (Shift+Tab back) weapon, Esc/P menu, R recover, C camera reset (3D only), F flaps, G gear, K countermeasures, L catapult, V fire (held). Multiplayer adds hold ` for the scoreboard, T/Enter for chat, and [ ] to spectate once destroyed. Unbound in flight: H I J O U Y, 7-0, Alt, CapsLock and the right mouse button (js/flight.js:989-1006; js/mpui.js:77-85)
- The context menu on #view is already suppressed by Builder (the same canvas element, and the listener has no scene guard), so a right click in flight brings up no menu (js/builder.js:1754; js/main.js:14-15)
- Window blur clears the held keys, the stick, touchBrake and touchFire (js/flight.js:1009)
- Canvas pointer handling: pointerdown has no e.button filter, so a one-pointer drag with any button calls Render3D.orbitBy (it pans in map mode). Two pointers pinch-zoom. A tap (moved under 10 px, under 450 ms) calls tapAt, which in 3D does pickPart, then usePart (toggles an engine, fires a separator, opens a chute) or switches vessel; in 2D it does hitTest (js/flight.js:1018-1049, 1160-1205)
- Wheel and pinch zoom change Flight.scale (clamped 0.00005..80); in map mode they zoom the map or globe instead (js/flight.js:1012-1016, 1133-1135)
- Touch stick: knob travel radius 0.36·width, 0.12 dead zone, up = +v (W: nose down / drive forward). ⟲⟳ set stick.r to ±1 while held. Brake is hold-to-brake. FIRE sets touchFire and fireTap (with a haptic). The weapon chip click cycles weapons, CM drops countermeasures, and dragging the throttle track sets the throttle from the pointer's Y (js/flight.js:1051-1112)
- Car on wheels: canDrive = !aircraft && a driven wheel touches. The drive command is ctl.v (not v.throttle). The target speed is ctl.v × min(wheel.speed); acceleration is clamped to ±max(wheel.accel), times grip. With no input the car slows gently. Braking works through rolling friction 0.8 (js/vessel.js:973-980, 994, 1035-1047)
- Car Wheel: speed 38 m/s, accel 5, r 0.54, drive:true; Rover Wheel: speed 12, accel 2.5; the Driver Cab (control 2, torque 4000) and two chassis are the 'ground' category (js/parts.js:110, 423-429)
- Steering is front wheels only: steer = ctl.h × min(0.6, 6·wheelbase/vF²) for non-planes. 'Front' means more than 0.3 m ahead of the COM along the forward axis. There is no skid or differential steering; Q/E (ctl.r) feeds the nose wheel only for planes (js/vessel.js:981-986, 998-999)
- The stick does not turn a craft on wheels: onWheels leaves every pilot rate null. With sas 'hold' and no input a car levels itself to the ground and gets righted when on its side. In the air PILOT.car maps W/S to pitch, A/D to roll and Q/E to yaw (js/vessel.js:504-544, 103)
- A car's forward is part +x (right on the blueprint) and its up is +y (PILOT.car and DRIVE.other) (js/vessel.js:103, 106)
- v.steer is computed but nothing outside vessel.js reads it, so the front wheels never visibly steer (handbook ch.2 §6 says the renderers read it). Wheels spin from p.spin (js/vessel.js:985; js/parts3d.js:1783-1792)
- Car autopilot is effectively dead UI: cars show the 6 rocket autopilot buttons. In a headless run the Dune Buggy stayed upright (tilt under 3°) when set to hold, prograde or radialOut at about 28 m/s (js/flight.js:228-235 (verified with a scratch script on test/lib/game.js))
- Follow cam: CAM0.car = [yaw 0, pitch 0.22], behind the nose (-pilot F). The heading follows the hull at a rate of 1-exp(-2.2·dt), and not while the nose points near vertical. Yaw = fc.follow + the player's fc.yaw, so a drag offset stays relative to the hull (js/render3d.js:2381-2393)
- Camera placement: dist = (H/2)/F.scale/TANH. The camera orbits the craft's position and calls lookAt(0,0,0), so the craft's centre is at the screen centre, with no look-ahead. It is pushed to at least ground + 2 m and above the carriers; the flutter shake rotates it (js/render3d.js:2412-2434)
- FOV is a constant 50° and TANH = tan(25°) is a constant, used by the camera distance and by metresPerPixelAt (World markers). The flight camera is a PerspectiveCamera(FOV, 1, 0.05, 5e12) (js/render3d.js:8, 485, 3327-3331)
- orbitBy: ty -= dx·0.006 and tp = clamp(tp + dy·0.006, -1.25, 1.45). resetCamera goes back to CAM0[kind] the short way round. The camera eases to ty/tp at a rate of 1-exp(-14·dt) (js/render3d.js:3349-3360, 2414-2415)
- Render3D.project, metresPerPixelAt and pickPart (RAY.setFromCamera from NDC) are the screen-to-world helpers available to HUD and aim code (js/render3d.js:3320-3348)
- Flight.updateCamera follows viewVessel (the spectated player or our own craft) body-relative and slerps viewQ by 0.25 a frame. The ghostCam silly flag freezes it after a crash (js/flight.js:1453-1468)
- App.frame flight branch: Flight.update every frame. The 3D path then draws: updateCamera → Render3D.renderFlight → clear2D → World.drawOverlay3D → Combat.drawHUD → MPUI.drawHUD (in a room). The 2D fallback calls only Flight.render, so there is no combat HUD. A paused flight or a dialog draws 10 fps (js/main.js:81-106)
- Combat.drawHUD returns at once without Render3D.ready. It draws target brackets, Bandit diamonds, the seeker circle and LOCK, a gun or rocket pipper 600 m along the nose with gravity drop, a LEAD marker (air targets only), the bomb impact point, MSL markers and the RWR (js/combat.js:1203-1298)
- updateHUD (every 0.08 s while not paused; set() writes only changed text): speed in km/h for everything but rockets; Mach when above 0.3 and not landed; AoA and G-load for planes only; heading for non-rockets on Earth; damage when integrity is at or below 99.5%; orbit rows hidden for non-rockets below 0.7 of the atmosphere; Ground when under 5 km. The weapon box, CM, FIRE and catapult visibility come from Combat.status; Brake shows when the craft has wheels or air brakes (js/flight.js:1314-1446, 580-581)
- For a drivable car with no stage left, the stage box reads 'Drive with W/S, steer A/D · hold B to brake', even on touch screens; the car hint is touch-aware (js/flight.js:1420, 1264-1267)
- The throttle box is always visible, but v.throttle only scales thrust parts; car wheels ignore it (js/flight.js:1208-1212; js/vessel.js:416, 978)
- Weapon systems: one per weapon part type, excluding flares, chaff and turrets. Guns fire along the fixed part axis (partAxis), toed in to meet 600 m ahead; there is no player-aimed weapon (js/combat.js:158-170, 243-256)
- Firing: Combat.step fires guns and rocket pods every physics tick while F.firing is set. playerFrame handles missile lock and the trigger edge (wasFiring/fireTap) for missiles and bombs (js/combat.js:398-418, 474-486)
- Turrets aim themselves: the nearest air target in range, with lead, slewing at 2.5 rad/s. On the ground or a catapult they park and hold fire. Aim is stored as p.aimYaw/p.aimEl, which the parts3d turret rig renders and multiplayer 'pv' rows already carry (js/combat.js:351-398; js/parts3d.js:1804; js/mp.js:629-632, 804)
- The weapon chip comes from Combat.status {name, ammo, n, lock, kills, flares, chaff, stealth, cmOnly}; cycle() changes v.wsel and clears the lock (js/combat.js:1429-1452; js/flight.js:1427-1433)
- HUD DOM: #flight-alert, .telemetry (t-grid rows: alt, ground, speed, mach, aoa, hdg, dmg, g, vs, Ap, Pe, Δv, heat, target, enc), #warp, .flight-top-right (Recover, Globe/System, Centre, Map, Menu), #map-legend, #hint, .throttle-box, .left-controls (.air-btns flaps/gear, #sas-bar, .rot-controls: #joy, roll buttons, #btn-brake), #btn-catapult, #weapon-box (chip, FIRE, CM), .stage-box (index.html:202-298)
- Only direct children of a .ui screen take pointer input; [hidden] uses display:none !important (css/style.css:36, 41-42)
- Desktop layout: telemetry top-left (214 px); warp top centre; top-right buttons; throttle at the right, vertically centred; left-controls bottom-left (autopilot grid 6×38 px, stick 116 px, roll buttons 52, brake 68 in the row); weapon box right+62 px, bottom 176; stage box bottom-right (220 px); hint bottom centre; flight-alert fixed, centred, top max(150 px, 22vh); toasts top 60 px; FIRE 58 px on pointer:fine (css/style.css:176-179, 188-198, 210-213, 226-241, 267-272, 276-280, 503-519, 733-745)
- Phone portrait (max-width 760): telemetry 176 px; warp under the top-right buttons; toasts at 100 px; stage box 176; round buttons 62; autopilot 3 columns; hint top-right at 104 px (at most 200 px wide); Brake moved absolute to left 146 px above the controls; stick 104; weapon box right+54, bottom 168; catapult bottom-left at 250 px; the room's bar pushes the hint and the kill feed down (css/style.css:338-368, 523-526, 562-567, 595-598, 745, 902-913)
- Phone landscape (max-height 500): compact telemetry; the throttle turns horizontal at the bottom, right+202 px; stage box 190; stick 92; round buttons 54 (small ones 42); autopilot 6×32 px; hint top centre at 60 px. The weapon box has no landscape rule (css/style.css:370-389, 535-540, 569-572, 599-603)
- No blur in flight: body.flying:not(.mapping) drops backdrop-filter only for .btn, .panel, .round, .stage-box, .sas, .joy and .pill; a new HUD class must be added to that list or it stays blurred and costs GPU time every frame (css/style.css:257-264)
- rwrPlace and MPUI.drawRadar measure the .telemetry and .left-controls rectangles (once a second) to place their canvas scopes; test/browser/rwr-hud.mjs checks the RWR placement on 5 screen sizes (js/combat.js:1389-1411; js/mpui.js:541-549; test/browser/rwr-hud.mjs:29)
- The controls are listed in three places: the README table, the App.showHelp howto (its 'Cars and helicopters' and 'Camera' items) plus Flight.controlsHelp, which is also the pause and multiplayer menu body. Other places name controls too: the launch tip and the vehicle hints. They already disagree slightly: R (recover) and P (pause) appear in neither the README nor controlsHelp (README.md:39-58; js/main.js:312-330, 281; js/flight.js:481-502, 1243-1275)
- Toasts: UI.toast removes a duplicate, keeps at most 4 and fades them at 2.6 s; Flight.toast wraps it. Hints come from Flight.hint and vehicleHint, gated by Settings 'hints' (js/ui.js:36-46; js/flight.js:190, 1214-1275)
- 2D fallback: used when WebGL is missing or failed, or with retro2d. World.render draws a side cut through the XY plane: vessels at (x, y) turned by v.rot, with z ignored, so a car turning north or south looks like it slows down or stops. No combat HUD is drawn (js/main.js:146-153, 103; js/world.js:317-392, 691-738)
- There is no pointer lock or gamepad code anywhere. In flight the input surface is the 2D canvas #view, stacked over #view3d (which only listens for webglcontextlost) (index.html:21-22; js/render3d.js:482)
- The traces baseline sets v.ctl directly (the buggy gets ctl.v, ctl.h and ctl.r on a fixed script), so changes to readControls cannot move the baselines, but any change to how Vessel reads ctl for 'car' will (test/traces.js:17-23)

## Constraints
- Old designs and saves must behave exactly as before. The Dune Buggy and any 'car' use vessel.js:973-1046 and the traces baseline drives v.ctl directly (test/traces.js:19), so put tank or track behaviour behind a new kind or new part flags, not in the 'car' code path.
- Keep the controls in sync in three places in the same commit: the README.md table (39-58), App.showHelp (main.js:312-330) and Flight.controlsHelp (flight.js:481-502). The launch tip (main.js:281), vehicleHint and the stage-next text name controls too.
- The phone layouts matter as much as the desktop one: portrait (max-width 760) and on its side (max-height 500) each have their own rules, the touch controls render on desktop too, and isTouch is fixed at load. Check a new button or panel on 390×844, 375×667, 844×390 and 1280×720 (the screens rwr-hud.mjs uses).
- Only direct children of #flight-ui take pointer events (style.css:41-42); show and hide with the hidden attribute (it beats CSS).
- Battery: no per-frame DOM layout. Move bars with transform: scaleX, use updateHUD's 0.08 s cadence and set() for text, and draw per-frame visuals on the 2D canvas. Add any new HUD class to the no-blur list at style.css:261 so it isn't backdrop-blurred over the moving 3D view.
- Frame-rate independence: turret slew, throttle or aim ramps and reload must scale with dt or h; the 60 fps cap only skips frames.
- Key handlers must ignore input while UI.modalOpen or while focus is in an INPUT/TEXTAREA. Escape is swallowed by the modal's capture listener (main.js:22).
- Multiplayer: never put peer text in innerHTML; Silly flags are off in PvP. Turret aim already travels in 'pv' rows (mp.js:629-632); changing that row, or adding pose fields an older peer would draw differently, needs a NET_VERSION bump. Combat.damage does nothing on remote craft (use MP.sendHit).
- Renderer: log depth buffer (custom shaders need the log-depth chunks; use depthBias, not polygonOffset) and a floating origin relative to F.camAbs. Any 3D reticle or tracer must respect both.
- 2D and 3D must agree where both draw the same thing. The 2D fallback has no combat HUD today, so decide explicitly what ground combat shows there.
- No modules and no fetch of local files. A new js file needs a script tag in dependency order, a sw.js SHELL entry, a CACHE bump and a README layout line. Check new top-level names for clashes across files.
- Player-visible names must not use real vehicle designations (altered names only).
- Control comes first: prefer warnings to taking control away. Keep the joke settings working in the new modes (the drunk stick affects ctl; ghostCam freezes the camera after a crash).

## Risks
- The follow camera's heading eases after the hull (render3d.js:2392) and the player's drag is only an offset on top of it. If the turret follows the camera, the aim will swing every time the hull turns unless a ground mode keeps the camera's yaw in the surface frame.
- The camera always looks at the craft's centre (render3d.js:2430), so the screen centre is the hull, not an aim point. A fixed centre crosshair would not match where the gun points without a look-ahead or offset camera, or a projected reticle.
- FOV and TANH are constants (render3d.js:8) used by the zoom distance and by metresPerPixelAt, which places World's 3D markers. A zoomed gunner sight that only changes camera.fov would misplace the markers and break the distance-from-scale formula.
- Pointer lock: the browser uses the first Esc to leave the lock (the page may not get it), clashing with Esc for the menu (flight.js:999). Clicks while locked reach #view with a fixed clientX/Y and would call tapAt → pickPart → usePart, toggling engines on whatever sits at that point. HUD buttons can't be clicked while locked, and iOS Safari doesn't support it.
- Canvas pointerdown ignores e.button, so a right-drag orbits the camera today; a 'hold RMB to zoom or aim' scheme has to change the drag handler. Shift, Ctrl, Z and X are throttle keys, so War Thunder's Shift for the sniper sight can't be copied as is.
- Keys 1-6 already change the autopilot for cars. Reusing them for ammo needs a kind-gated map (like PLANE_SAS_KEYS); otherwise one key press changes both the autopilot and the ammo.
- Repurposing Q/E (ctl.r) for turret traverse is safe on wheels but changes air control (yaw in the air) and the planes' rudder-steer path. Keep it per kind; the buggy trace feeds ctl.r between 9 and 10 s.
- The phone screen is already full: right column throttle (centred), weapon box above the stage box, top-right buttons and hint; left side autopilot grid, stick, roll buttons and Brake (absolute at left 146 px in portrait). A sight button, ammo picker, reload bar and a second aim stick need room in both orientations, and the weapon box has no landscape rule (FIRE 72 px + CM 56 px + chip above bottom 176 px on a 390 px-tall screen).
- New panels near the telemetry or left-controls change where the RWR scope and the room's radar scope go (they measure those rectangles), and test/browser/rwr-hud.mjs may fail.
- The wheels never visibly steer (v.steer is unread) and tracks don't exist; ground movement work needs render-side changes too, or the owner will spot wheels sliding.
- The 2D fallback ignores z, so a tank fight there mostly shows nothing useful. Combat.drawHUD is skipped in 2D, so a gunner sight would be blank.
- Dead or irrelevant HUD for ground kinds (the always-shown throttle box, the rocket autopilot buttons, a Stage button that is often disabled) wastes phone space; hiding it per kind changes the layout the RWR placement measures.
- App.frame swallows exceptions (main.js:113-115), so a bug in a new sight or camera mode can fail silently every frame; watch the console in browser checks.
