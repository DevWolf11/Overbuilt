# Terrain, world and sites (js/terrain.js, js/space.js, js/world.js, combat.js target layout and line of sight)

Earth is a 315 km sphere. Ground positions use the space centre's frame: e is metres east of the pad along the equator, z is latitude·R, and game north is −z. siteAngle(EARTH, e) = padAngle − e/R. Combat's at(e,z,h) turns that into sim xyz, and Terrain.locate goes back (a, z), with e = −wrapAngle(a−padAngle)·R.

Physics reads the ground only through Terrain.surfaceAt, heightAt, waterAt, normalAt and deckAt. On the track (|z| ≤ trackHalf(e): 25 m, 45 m on the runway, 260 m at the pad), the height is exactly the 2D profile terrainHeight, with flats for the pad, runway and causeway. Off the track it is a bilinear 2 m lattice cache over h3dAt. With the real map, h3dAt combines the map height, hills, coast noise, the ISLE island, 26 m sea cliffs on the Faroes and Shetland, rivers, roads and lakes, and flattened airfields. Only terrain and carrier decks are solid: buildings, trees and towns are drawn only.

Measured with the real map:
- **Faroes land:** about 3.5 × 2.8 km.
- **Runway:** sits in a shallow cut, flat at 40 m, with 60-64 m ridges 250-450 m to each side.
- **Shetland:** about 2.5 × 5 km, 0-65 m high. Both islands end in cliffs.
- **North Scotland (e 15.5-20 km, z 13.5-17 km):** rolling ground 40-150 m high, 93% of it under 10°. It lies just south of the existing Scotland FFA airfield and is the best open arena.

Curvature limits ground-to-ground sight to about 2.1 km over flat ground. combat.js clearLine (4 m margin, about 150 m samples) says the line is blocked even on the flat runway at 500 m, so it cannot be used between ground vehicles.

Range targets spawn only in single player. Nearly all land targets end up on Shetland, some even on its airstrip.

Spawns are {e, z, yaw} handed to Flight.start and then to placeOnSurface. A capture point is just (e, z, r), checked with hypot(Δe, Δz) after Terrain.locate.

## Key facts
- EARTH: radius 315000 m, terrainBase 40, padAngle π/2, padHeight 40, ocean true; terrainMax = sum of profile amps + |terrainBase| = 352 before the map loads (js/space.js:55-63, js/space.js:13)
- Sites in metres east of the pad: pad 0, helipad 160, runway 760 (planes AND cars start here), bay −2300 (float planes, facing π). Paved runway 700..2700; causeway raised 3300..17200, ashore at 17850 (js/space.js:69-76)
- EARTH.flats (spaceCentre e0 half220 h40, causeway h12, runway e1700 half1100 h40; later entries win), each with a cached .ang; terrainHeight starts from the last flat holding the point, so terrainRaw (slow on the map) is never called on pad, runway or causeway (js/space.js:79-84, js/space.js:184-197)
- Airfields: shetland {e18600, z−1650, yaw π/2, hl750, hw30, blend260, h40}, scotland {e18900, z12500, yaw π/3, hl1100, hw30, blend300, h20}. Carriers west/east/south: deck 18 m, hl130, hw18, cat95. c = cos(yaw) and s = −sin(yaw) are cached, so the nose goes (cos yaw, −sin yaw) in (e, z): yaw 0 = east, π/2 = game north (−z) (js/space.js:88-99)
- siteAngle(b, east) = b.padAngle − east/b.radius (east is decreasing angle) (js/space.js:101)
- Real-Earth placement: SITE {lat 62.16, lon −7.33, head 119°} puts the pad on the Faroes' west coast. GM rotation; toGeo returns the shared GEO singleton (copy it); toSim is the inverse; bearingAt gives the compass (js/terrain.js:91-118, js/terrain.js:140-146)
- trackHalf(e): 25 m by default, 260 m at the pad, 45 m along the runway, 14 m on the causeway. trackBlend 150/330/230. APRONS e820..1330, z−165..−18 (js/terrain.js:57-69)
- setEarthData sets EARTH.terrainMax = 1700 and EARTH.mapped = true and clears the cfgs/hc/hwc caches and the earthRaw memo. loadEarth failure (always on file://) keeps the generated terrain (js/terrain.js:152-200 (176-178))
- Mapped-Earth height recipe in natural(): map height + hillW·short profile octaves + coastN + island() (ISLE e1500, a2000, b650, h44: only adds land) + detail noise, then cliffs() (+26 m cliff where land meets sea on the Faroes and Shetland, not within 300-900 m of the causeway harbour), then features() (rivers carve, roads levelled to the map's smooth height, lakes) (js/terrain.js:83, 121-136, 304-335, 376-394, 521-527)
- height(): blends to the track height by builtW (trackHalf/trackBlend plus aprons), then flattens airfields via fieldAt (FQ shared; w = 1 on the strip, fading over blend). Airfields flatten even without the map (js/terrain.js:538-559, 586-597)
- Physics lattice: GS = 2 m; hc (heights) and hwc (inland water) are Maps keyed iz·n+ia, cleared wholesale above 80000 entries or on a body change. heightAt and surfaceAt return terrainHeight exactly on the track (onTrack), else a bilinear lattice. surfaceAt returns a carrier deck (deckAt, DECK_PLAN polygon) first, the sea (h<0 → 0), inland water above the ground, then the ground (js/terrain.js:606-661)
- locate(b, rx, ry, rz) → {a, lat, z = lat·R, r}; normalAt uses 1.5 m central differences of surfaceAt (not cached; a deck is flat) (js/terrain.js:663-680)
- Terrain exports (the full API surface) (js/terrain.js:682-687)
- Vessel ground contact: the broad phase uses reach + terrainMax + deck. Each hull point and wheel rim near the ground calls locate + surfaceAt; each penetrating one adds normalAt (4 more surfaceAt), 2× deckAt and waterAt. The Dune Buggy makes 23-27 terrain calls per 1/120 s step (measured) (js/vessel.js:931-951)
- Driving and righting read Terrain.normalAt under the craft; groundAltitude/groundHeight/surfaceSpot use locate + surfaceAt (js/vessel.js:350-364, js/vessel.js:535-541)
- placeOnSurface(body, ang, rot, z0, yaw): sets q to the radial (not the ground-normal) frame turned by yaw, and lifts the craft so its lowest hull/wheel point is 0.02 m above surfaceAt at the centre point ONLY. On a slope, one end can start inside the ground (js/vessel.js:1069-1082)
- Flight.start places at opts.place {e, z, yaw, catapult, soft} through siteAngle(EARTH, P.e), else at EARTH.sites[VEHICLE_KINDS[kind].site] (car → 'runway') (js/flight.js:28-51, js/parts.js:25-30)
- MP spawns: MP_SPAWNS (faroes base team 0 = the runway; shetland team 1; scotland FFA; three carriers). placement() turns a spawn + slot into {e, z, yaw}; airfieldPlacement puts vehicles on the strip near the threshold, 230 m apart. MP_KINDS_PVP = plane, heli, car (js/mp.js:16, js/mp.js:21-28, js/mp.js:571-588, js/mp.js:938-958)
- surfaceFrame(x,y,z): up radial, east = up×(0,0,1), north = east×up (= +z = game SOUTH) (js/util.js:57-63)
- Combat position helpers: at(e,z,h) → sim xyz; groundAt = surfaceAt and rawAt = heightAt at (siteAngle(e), z) (js/combat.js:895-900)
- TARGET_KINDS (tank hp600 r3.6 speed4 land; truck; radar; bunker; aa; sam; boat; ship; drone) and TARGET_LAYOUT counts (js/combat.js:8-19)
- landOk: raw height 2..220 m; not |z|<140 for e −900..shore+400; not within 2200 m of (1300,0); flat within 12 m over 25 m. place(): land is 45% Shetland (e 17200..20800, |z|<3000), else Faroes (e −2500..4000, |z|<4000); sea e 2500..16500 with depth < −12. Seeded RNG; 400 tries (js/combat.js:902-920)
- Targets spawn only when the player is on Earth within 60 km of at(8000,0,0), with targets mode on and the map ready; they are cleared otherwise. mode() is 'off' in any MP room. Dead targets respawn elsewhere after 60 s (js/combat.js:27-28, js/combat.js:1107-1118)
- Hostile AA/SAM/frigate engage only a 'flying' player (!landed && altitude > 40; SAMs > 150 m), so ground vehicles are ignored today (js/combat.js:1121-1139)
- syncTarget: moving ground targets step along heading and test landOk each step (bounce off bad ground); static ones cache pos while e/z are unchanged (js/combat.js:936-960)
- clearLine(o, p, d): n = clamp(ceil(d/150), 4, 120) interior samples; blocked if any sample is below ground (sea counts as 0) + 4 m. Used by siteSees (frigate radar, memoised for 0.25 s on the missile) (js/combat.js:718-737)
- moveShot ground hit: every shot, every physics substep (Combat.step at flight.js:642 loops S.shots at combat.js:499-501), does locate + surfaceAt at the step's end point (no altitude early-out), then explodes, splashes or puffs. Segment-vs-ground is not tested, only the end point (js/combat.js:588-608)
- Space-centre buildings: VAB x(e) −115..−67, z −110..−50; mission control e −160..−126, z −90..−55; placed with surf(e, h, z), which follows the curvature. 2D draws the same buildings. Render-only: no collision (js/render3d.js:1464-1467, js/render3d.js:1523, js/render3d.js:1541, js/world.js:584-633)
- 2D fallback draws each vessel at its sim x,y only (z ignored) against the z=0 terrain profile, so a craft off the track is drawn about z²/2R too low (≈4 m at the Shetland airfield, ≈250 m at z 12.5 km) (js/world.js:691-699)
- MEASURED (real map, headless): runway |z|<40 is exactly 40 m. Cross-section at e1700: 40 m at |z|≤50, rising to 60-64 m at |z| 250-450 (a cut about 20 m deep), ~6° side slopes. Runway sides: 95% of the ground under 10°, max 35°. Space centre: 93% under 5° (scratchpad terr3.js/terr6.js (terrain.js surfaceAt))
- MEASURED land extents: Faroes ≈ e −500..3000 × z −2300..+500, plus a spit to z 2750 at e 2000-2500, 0-76 m high, 56% of it under 5°, 7% over 20° (cliffs). Shetland ≈ e 17.5..20.3 km × z −2.5..+2.25 km, 0-65 m high: <5° 50%, 5-10° 23%, 10-20° 15%, 20-30° 9%, >30° 3.5%. Shetland strip 39-41 m flat (scratchpad terr1.js/terr3.js)
- MEASURED: Scotland FFA airfield area (e 17.8-20 km, z 11.4-13.6 km) is 90% land, 0-43 m, 88% under 5°: flat, little short-range cover. South of it, North Scotland e 15.5-20 km × z 13.5-17 km is rolling 40-150 m ground, 57% under 5°, 36% 5-10°, max 30°, with roads, no lochs. Highlands e 19-27 km × z 16-25 km reach 250 m and have lochs (inland water) and towns (Inverness 1.9 km from e22000 z19000) (scratchpad terr2.js/terr3.js/terr7.js/terr11.js)
- MEASURED cover (fine line of sight, gun 2.2 m to target 1.3 m, 5 m steps, 0.3 m margin), share of land points hidden at 200-500 / 500-800 / 800-1200 m: runway 43/70/67%; Shetland airfield 67/61/77%; Scotland airfield 19/53/57%; North Scotland e18500 z14000 46/46/71%, e17000 z15000 54/77/79% (scratchpad terr5.js/terr11.js)
- MEASURED curvature: drop d²/2R = 1.6 m at 1 km, 3.6 m at 1.5 km, 6.4 m at 2 km. Flat-ground sight between a 2.2 m gun and a 1.3 m target is at most ≈2.08 km (two 2.5 m eyes: 2.5 km). On the runway, 1.5 km is visible and 1.9 km is not (scratchpad terr5.js)
- MEASURED clearLine between ground vehicles (2.2 m and 1.3 m up) on the flat runway: false at 500 m and at 1500 m; true only when both ends are ≥ ~4.5 m above the ground. It reports 81-100% of ground pairs blocked where true line of sight shows 46-89% (js/combat.js:730-737; scratchpad terr4.js)
- MEASURED cost (Node VM): uncached h3dAt ≈ 48 µs; cached surfaceAt ≈ 4.7 µs; surfaceAt walking new ground ≈ 125 µs per point; moveShot's ground check with a new point every 8.3 m (a 1000 m/s shell at 120 Hz) ≈ 150 µs per step over land, 96 µs over sea (scratchpad terr3.js/terr4.js)
- MEASURED: the stock Dune Buggy stalls on Shetland grades of 20-37% (11-20°) and stops in a Highlands loch (surfaceAt returns the lake level, so water damping applies) (scratchpad terr8.js/terr9.js)
- MEASURED: in practice nearly all land range targets land on Shetland. The Faroes' land is mostly inside the 2.2 km exclusion round (1300, 0). Tanks were placed ON the Shetland airstrip (e18653 z−1515), because landOk excludes only the Faroes track (js/combat.js:902-920; scratchpad terr10.js)
- Without the map (file://), the geography differs completely: Faroes north (e1000 z−1500) is sea at −62 m, Shetland mid is 153 m, and only the flats and airfields (fieldAt) keep their heights (scratchpad terr12.js; js/terrain.js:592-595)

It is inside if hypot(e − cap.e, z − cap.z) < cap.r (the e scale error at |z| ≤ 20 km is under 0.2%). Ground height for markers: Terrain.surfaceAt(EARTH, siteAngle(EARTH, e), z). The lattice and renderer pick it up automatically (height() is shared by physics and patchJob). Raised berms would need a new term in height() and must stay off every existing site, track and range-target area (seeded landOk placement would move), or the baselines shift
- Solid cover (walls, buildings, wrecks) for shells and vehicles @ js/terrain.js deckAt (563-585), the only non-terrain solid; consumed by surfaceAt/normalAt/waterAt and Vessel.ground (vessel.js:935-949): A deckAt-like 'block' layer (axis-aligned or polygon footprints with a top height) could feed surfaceAt so vehicles and shells collide. It must keep the same hit point for anything that hits, to keep combat baselines and old behaviour

## Constraints
- Never change existing terrain inputs (EARTH.flats, sites, runway/causeway, airfields, carriers, ISLE, CLIFFS, HLUT, trackHalf/trackBlend, APRONS, LOOK). They decide where every old design starts and what it touches; traces.js and handling.js start on the pad and runway, and range-target placement is seeded off landOk. Add new entries away from them instead
- On the track, heightAt and surfaceAt must stay exactly terrainHeight(b, a). The runway's crisp edges and the 2D/3D agreement depend on it
- Shared return values: Terrain.toGeo (GEO), Terrain.water (EW), lineQuery (LQ), fieldAt (FQ), Terrain.dark(), bodyPos/bodyVel, v.surfaceSpot() (_loc). Copy them before another call, and read side results only right after your own height()/lineQuery call (the earthRaw memo relies on this)
- The lattice cache (hc/hwc) is cleared wholesale at 80000 entries (≈0.32 km² of 2 m cells). Many vehicles spread over km, plus per-step shell checks and line-of-sight sampling, will thrash it. Uncached cells cost ~48 µs each (more on a phone)
- The 'east' coordinate is in metres along the equator at the pad: siteAngle(EARTH, e) = padAngle − e/R. Game north is −z; surfaceFrame().north is +z (game south); placement yaw 0 faces east and π/2 faces game north
- EARTH.terrainMax is 352 without the map and 1700 with it; the broad-phase ground checks and the prediction's impact test depend on it
- The real map may be missing (file://, failed load). The geography is then totally different (e.g. the Faroes north lobe is sea), and only flats and airfields keep their heights. Range targets wait for the map; App.afterEarth makes launches wait while it loads. A ground mode that depends on real hills must wait for EM.state === 'ready', or refuse to start without it
- Multiplayer: every peer computes spawn positions locally from MP_SPAWNS and slots (mp.js:571-588), so arena data must be identical everywhere. Bump NET_VERSION if positions or what a peer builds or hits change. AI, range targets and auto-flares are off in any room (combat.js:27-28)
- 2D and 3D must agree, but the 2D fallback is a side view along z = 0 and draws craft by x/y only (world.js:691-699). An off-track arena is already drawn wrong in 2D (≈250 m low at z 12.5 km); decide whether to accept or handle it
- Battery and phones: line-of-sight checks and capture-zone tests must be throttled (siteSees memoises for 0.25 s) and not run per frame per pair; per-step terrain lookups per vehicle are ~23-27 calls today
- combat.test.js baselines the frigate radar SAM (siteSees and clearLine) and blasts; clearLine's 4 m margin and 150 m sampling must not change for radars
- Sea cliffs (+26 m) ring the Faroes and Shetland (except within ~300-900 m of the causeway harbour at e 17850), so vehicles can only leave an island by the causeway (14 m half-width, 12 m high, 14 km long)

## Risks
- Planet curvature (R = 315 km) hides a ground target beyond about 2.1 km over flat ground (1.6 m drop at 1 km, 6.4 m at 2 km). Tank-destroyer, ATGM and SAM 'long shots' at ground targets beyond about 2 km are impossible unless the shooter is on high ground; this is fine for War Thunder-like ranges (200-2000 m) but should be designed around
- combat.js clearLine is unusable between ground vehicles: with its 4 m margin, two vehicles on the flat runway 500 m apart are 'blocked'. With ~150 m sampling it also misses crests narrower than 150 m. Reusing it for ground AI or locks would make everything invisible
- Projectile cost: every shot does an uncached surfaceAt at each 1/120 s step (≈150 µs over land, measured in Node; slower on phones). Ground combat with machine guns and many shells near the ground could cost tens to hundreds of ms per second and thrash the 80000-entry lattice cache
- placeOnSurface samples the ground only at the centre point and aligns the craft to the radial up, not the ground normal. A long vehicle spawned on a slope starts partly buried at the uphill end, and the 0.9× push-out can jolt it. Spawn pads must be flat or flattened
- Inland water (lochs, rivers) returns the water level from surfaceAt, and vehicles without floats are damped there (the stock buggy stopped dead in a Highlands loch). The Highlands, and anything near rivers, are a trap for tanks
- Cliffs and steep coast (up to 45-72° on 4 m baselines; 3.5-8.5% of Shetland and Faroes land is over 30°) can flip or strand vehicles. The stock buggy already stalls on 20-37% grades on Shetland
- Nothing but terrain and carrier decks is solid. Space-centre buildings, airfield hangars, towns and trees are render-only, so 'cover' behind a building does not stop shells and vehicles drive through walls. Players will notice
- Range targets in single player sit almost entirely on Shetland, some on its airstrip. A ground practice mode there would mix with them, and AA, SAMs and fighters currently ignore landed or low (<40 m) craft
- Changing heights near Shetland or the Faroes (new flats or berms) moves seeded range-target positions and possibly scenery and paving, and could move test baselines
- The 2D fallback (no WebGL or retro2d) draws off-track ground vehicles far below or above its ground line

## Open questions
- Which arena should the ground mode use? (a) North Scotland rolling ground south of the existing Scotland FFA airfield (e 15.5-20 km, z 13.5-17 km; recommended: about 4.5 × 3.5 km, 40-150 m, few cliffs, no lochs, good cover). (b) Shetland south (existing team-1 base, about 2.5 × 5 km, cliff-ringed, where the range targets live). (c) The Faroes island round the runway cut (the team-0 base, about 3 × 3 km, buildings not solid). (d) The Highlands (more relief, but lochs and towns)
- Should cover objects (walls, buildings, wrecks) be made solid through a deckAt-like layer, or should cover be terrain only?
- Must ground battles work without the real Earth map (file://, failed load), or should the mode wait for or refuse without it?
- Should engagement and spotting use true line of sight (a new helper), and should the curvature horizon of about 2 km be accepted, or should arenas be chosen or shaped so that high ground gives longer shots?
- Should single-player range AA, SAMs and frigates start engaging ground vehicles (they ignore anything under 40 m or landed today), and should range tanks and trucks fire back?
- Should range-target placement (landOk) exclude the Shetland airstrip and any new arena, so ground spawns and capture points don't overlap targets?
- Is the 2D fallback view in scope for ground battles off the track (z ≠ 0)?
