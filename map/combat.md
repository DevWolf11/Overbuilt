# COMBAT (js/combat.js): weapons, projectiles, hits, damage, AI targets, SAMs, guidance, countermeasures and HUD, and where a War Thunder-style ground-combat layer would plug in

combat.js is one IIFE singleton, `Combat`, with its module state in `S` (combat.js:25). Flight calls `step(h,F)` on every 1/120 s physics substep (flight.js:642) and `frame(F,dt)` once per rendered frame (flight.js:538). Weapons are parts with `def.weapon.kind` set to gun, rocket, missile, bomb, turret, flare or chaff. `systems()` groups the trigger-fired kinds by part type. Turrets, flares and chaff are left out of that grouping because they work by themselves.

Fixed guns fire along the part's own axis (`partAxis`). Guns that sit off the centre line toe in to meet at 600 m, and the HUD pipper sits at that point along the hull's `noseDir`. Turrets aim themselves, only at air candidates and only while the craft is airborne. They hold fire whenever the craft touches the ground (PR #37). Their aim is stored as `p.aimYaw`/`p.aimEl` and replicated to other players.

Every round, rocket, missile and bomb is a plain object in `S.shots`, and `moveShot` steps it each substep:
1. gravity, plus one drag constant per kind;
2. a segment test against axis-aligned part boxes (`hitVessel`), which returns only the first part hit, with no face or normal;
3. a sphere test against range targets;
4. a ground test.

Damage is one hp pool per part. The only armour is the scalar `def.armor` (fuselageArmor 0.6), applied as `(1-armor)`. `explode` damages every part within the blast radius, falling off as (1-d/r)^1.5, with no shielding. Hits on another player's craft go to its owner through `MP.sendHit(s)`. `damage()` does nothing on a remote craft.

The single-player range (`TARGET_KINDS`) is made of spheres with hp. Tanks and trucks only wander about. Only the AA guns, SAM sites and the frigate shoot back, and only at an airborne player. AI fighters are built as real Vessels (`spawnFighter`).

There is no notion today of:
- shell types, penetration, effective thickness, ricochet or spall;
- player-aimed turrets, or traverse and elevation limits;
- ground-vs-ground AI.

Missile guidance is proportional navigation (`guideAir`) for air seekers, and lead pursuit with no decoys (`guide`) for ground seekers.

## Key facts
- Module state singleton S holds shots, targets, flares, kills, lock, warn, threats. S.killFeed is declared but never used anywhere: the kill feed lives in MP.feed and MPUI #mp-feed. (js/combat.js:25)
- mode() is forced to 'off' in any MP room (mpOn), so a room never has range targets, SAMs or AI fighters. Otherwise it reads Settings 'targets' (practice, hostile or off). (js/combat.js:27-29)
- TARGET_KINDS defines tank (hp 600, r 3.6, speed 4), truck (220, speed 9), radar (emitter), bunker (1600), aa (aa, emitter), sam (sam, emitter), boat, ship (hp 3200, aa, rsam, radar 13000) and drone (air, 75 m/s). TARGET_LAYOUT gives the counts. (js/combat.js:8-19)
- maxHp(def) = round((60+220*sqrt(mass+fuel*0.25))*(control?1.4:1)). p.hp is created lazily. engineK and liftK degrade thrust and lift with damage. (js/combat.js:37-41)
- damage() returns early for v.remote (the owner applies its own damage). Its only armour is p.hp -= amount*(1-def.armor). It sets lastHitBy and lastHitAt, toasts at 60% and 30%, and calls destroyPart at 0 hp. (js/combat.js:43-55)
- The only armour in the game is fuselageArmor, armor: 0.6, a scalar fraction (it also has breakSpeed 16). (js/parts.js:180)
- destroyPart sends parts with thrust, fuel over 50 kg, stores (rocket, missile or bomb ammo; gun ammo does not count) or control to sim.breakParts (an explosion), and everything else to ripParts. Fuel over 500 kg and stores add secondary blasts with by=null. A legacy twin pair blasts once at the centre. (js/combat.js:58-69)
- The craft is lost only when its control part goes, or all its parts do (breakParts/ripParts, then onDestroyed). There is no crew-knockout or ammo-rack logic. (js/flight.js:258-280, js/flight.js:372-389)
- boxesOf builds one axis-aligned box per copy from partRect, with depth max(w,0.6)/2 for twins, track/2+0.3 for wheels, else partDepth/2. The box does not rotate with a turret's aim. (js/combat.js:82-85)
- segBox is a slab test that returns only the entry fraction t0. The axis of the entry face, which would give the face normal, is computed internally but discarded. (js/combat.js:86-98)
- hitVessel returns only the FIRST part along the segment ({p,t}), after an early-out at reach+2 from the vessel's centre. It allocates arrays through boxesOf on every call. (js/combat.js:106-116)
- explode(): per-part falloff dmg*(1-d/blast)^1.5, where d is the distance to the nearest copy minus max(w,h)/2. Nothing shields anything. For our own fire, blasts of 8 m or more go out as MP.sendBoom, and remote craft collect [part,dmg] lists that go out through MP.sendHits(...,'blast'). Range targets are skipped in MP. (js/combat.js:128-154)
- systems() groups weapon parts by p.type, excludes flare, chaff and turret, and caches the result on v._ws keyed by parts.length. v.wsel is the selected system, and cycle() changes it (Tab). (js/combat.js:158-170, js/combat.js:1429-1435)
- rangeOf: a radar missile reaches min(range, own radar), or 5 km with no radar. An IR missile reaches range*(1+irst). (js/combat.js:178)
- muzzle(p, side) gives the release point in part space: a bayRack slot, the bay belly, a pod or store offset w*0.12, or a gun's tip at h/2. (js/combat.js:196-204)
- fireGun shoots along partAxis(p) (the part's +y, turned by rot). Off-centre muzzles (|z|>0.3) toe in by -m.z/600. Spread is ±s per axis. Rounds are k:'bullet', life 3 s, with a tracer every 3rd (every round for blast shells). net=true makes show rounds with dmg 0. (js/combat.js:242-264)
- fireRocket fires along the part axis at w.speed with the motor running from t=0. launchMissile drops the missile +4 m/s 'down' only for planes (zero for any other kind); there is no motor or guidance for the first 0.35 s; enemy is !!v.ai; MP.sendMissile. dropBomb does the same with +2 m/s. (js/combat.js:265-302)
- turretStep aims at the nearest candidates('air') within w.range, leading it by tof=d/w.speed. It converts into the turret's unturned frame by k=p.rot&3 rotations, with yaw=atan2(lz,ly) about part x and el=atan2(-lx,hypot(ly,lz)). It engages only if el > -0.12 and clamps el to ≥0. Both axes slew at 2.5 rad/s. gunOn is set within 0.06 rad, and then it calls turretFire. (js/combat.js:355-375)
- The grounded rule: v.contactTime>0, or held on a catapult, parks the guns at yaw 0, el 0 with no fire. It applies to every kind:'turret' part, including the retired 'turret'. (js/combat.js:356-359)
- turretFire rebuilds the gun direction from aimEl and aimYaw (gx=-sin el, gy=cos el*cos yaw, gz=cos el*sin yaw), turns it back by k, and spawns the round at the part centre +1.5 m along dir (not a real muzzle). Spread is (rand-0.5)*s, half of fireGun's. Life 2.5 s, tracer every 2nd round. (js/combat.js:377-394)
- Turret part defs: turret (retired) and turretL (manned): ammo 800, rate 12, speed 900, dmg 8, spread .006, range 1100. (js/parts.js:395-400)
- The turret 3D rig: yaw node rotation.x = p.aimYaw, pitch node rotation.z = p.aimEl. The base sits on the part's +x side. (js/parts3d.js:1804, js/parts3d.js:882-893, js/parts3d.js:627-647)
- aimYaw and aimEl (rounded to 0.01 rad), ammo, deployed and gunOn go to other players in 'pv' rows at 5 Hz (MP_PV_MS 200), only when changed (all of them every 2 s), and are applied in onPartVis. (js/mp.js:622-635, js/mp.js:796-810)
- playerFrame: missile lock picks the candidate nearest noseDir(v) (the hull's pilot.F) within the cone, range and minimum range (350 m air, 150 m ground). Lock takes 0.9 s IR or 1.4 s radar (×2 against a jammer). Missiles and bombs release on the trigger's rising edge or F.fireTap. (js/combat.js:397-431)
- noseDir(v) = v.axis(pilot.F). pilot.F is +y for planes and rockets, +x for cars and helis. So on a car a gun must be rotated (rot 3) to point forward, and the pipper and lock cone follow the hull. (js/combat.js:432, js/vessel.js:98-106)
- candidates(): 'air' takes AI bandits, air drones, and enemy ghosts that are not landed and not cars. Ground and radar seekers take non-air targets (radar: emitters only), plus enemy ghosts that are landed or kind==='car'. (js/combat.js:454-465)
- step(): runs doorStep for our own craft, fires guns while the trigger is held, ripples rockets, sends gun on/off over MP (sendGun) and runs netGuns. It then fires the AI's guns, runs turrets on F.active only (v.turrets=0 short-circuits), the jammer's missile-loss roll and aaFire for firing targets, and moves shots and flares. (js/combat.js:474-504)
- netGuns fires show rounds for remote guns (P.gun[type]) and for turrets with p.gunOn. (js/combat.js:506-518)
- moveShot step 1: central gravity b.mu/r². Drag kd=k*rho*spd*h, with k = s.drag only for air missiles; otherwise bullet 1.2e-4, bomb 3e-5, else 6e-5. There is no per-shell ballistic coefficient. (js/combat.js:522-547)
- moveShot hit order: vessels (the owner is skipped for t<1.5 s; replicas with s.net test nothing), then range targets (segPoint < def.r around targetCentre), then a dud if t<arm (10% damage), then the missile proximity fuse at blast*0.45. A direct hit calls explode() if s.blast, else MP.sendHit for a remote craft or damage() for a local one. (js/combat.js:549-587)
- moveShot ground or sea: Terrain.locate and surfaceAt on every substep. A cluster bomb bursts at 150 m and a torpedo turns into k:'torp'. Anything with blast explodes. A blastless bullet vanishes, with a puff or splash 35% of the time. There is no ricochet. (js/combat.js:588-610)
- airframe() returns {} for non-air seekers, so ground and radar missiles use guide(): lead pursuit with tgo≤12 s, turn rate turn*g/v, vmax as a hard cap, and no decoys, gimbal or energy model. guideAir is PN with gimbal, track rate, SARH radar checks, decoys and g-bleed. (js/combat.js:664-669, js/combat.js:765-816)
- radarSees(L,t,tp) uses the launcher's radarOf (or 5000), a 60° cone around noseDir(L), and clutter (a target low and under 40 m/s along the beam is 'notch'). siteSees is the all-round ship radar from mastOf, with clearLine terrain LOS sampled every ~150 m (4-120 samples) and 4 m clearance, Earth only. (js/combat.js:692-737)
- decoyed(): flares are weighed against irSig; the free chaff cloud and the dispenser bundle (str 2) have their own odds by chaffOdds/chaffR. Countermeasures by hand go through countermeasures() (0.6 s gate); autoCM is used by the AI and by solo RWR. (js/combat.js:437-451, js/combat.js:742-763, js/combat.js:305-349)
- Multiplayer replicas: netShot maps any k other than 'bomb' to a 'rocket' with blast 9 and dmg 0. netMissile and netMissileUpdate dead-reckon the other players' missiles. netFlares and netBoom exist. (js/combat.js:821-873)
- hitTarget subtracts dmg from e.hp (no armour). At 0 it sets alive=false, and when by===sim.active it adds S.kills++ and toasts '<name> destroyed'. aiDown credits 'Bandit shot down!'. (js/combat.js:876-893)
- Target placement and movement: place() puts land targets on Shetland (45%) or the Faroes, checked with landOk (height 2-220 m, off the runway and space centre, gentle slope). syncTarget wanders land and sea targets, turning randomly every 12-32 s at def.speed and bouncing off land that is not OK. Static targets cache pos. Drones orbit. (js/combat.js:895-960)
- Only aa, sam and ship targets shoot. aaFire: 850 m/s tracers, dmg 6, lead with no gravity term. samFire: an IR missile, first after 4 s then every 22 s. shipSamFire: a radar SAM riding siteSees. All of them target only F.active and are gated by flying = !v.landed && altitude>40 (SAMs and the frigate also by altitude>150). (js/combat.js:962-993, js/combat.js:1121-1140)
- spawnFighter builds a real Vessel from RAIDER rows (makePart, buildAdjacency, computeStages, rebuildGroups) with v.ai set. flyFighter's tgtOk requires !pl.landed || pl.speed()>20, so AI ignores a parked ground vehicle. (js/combat.js:996-1071)
- frame(): spawns and clears the range within 60 km of at(8000,0,0), respawns dead targets after 60 s, runs the hostile site loop and fighters, the RWR threats and auto CM (solo), cmFX, playerFrame, MP.sendLock, and smoke, fire and leaks on parts under 60%, 30% and 50%. (js/combat.js:1107-1191)
- drawHUD (3D view only; main.js:101 calls it after Render3D.renderFlight): target brackets (red if armed AA/SAM in hostile), Bandit diamonds, the seeker circle and LOCK, a gun or rocket pipper 600 m along noseDir with gravity drop and no drag, a LEAD marker on the nearest air candidate under 2 km, the bombsight via bombImpact (a stepped ballistic integrator), MSL markers within 4 km, then drawRWR. (js/combat.js:1203-1299, js/combat.js:1413-1426, js/main.js:101-102)
- status(v) feeds the weapon box: name (w.label or SHORT), ammo, n, lock, kills, flares, chaff and stealth. A craft with no system shows TURRET, FLARES, CHAFF or CM. Rendered by flight.js updateHUD. (js/combat.js:1438-1452, js/flight.js:1422-1433)
- MP damage path: MP.sendHits refuses non-hostile targets, sends {t:'hit', list:[[partId, dmg]], k} and adds to the damage score. onHit on the victim caps the list at 64 rows and each dmg at 5000, sets lastHitBy, and calls Combat.damage (which applies def.armor again on the receiver's side). k is only 'blast' or 'shot'. (js/mp.js:845-875)
- Kill credit: onLocalDestroyed sends 'down' with by = lastHitBy within 20 s. onDown adds Combat.S.kills++ and a toast for the killer. The host's recordDown tracks kills and deaths and calls feed('A ⟶ B'). checkWin and finish('time') rank by players alive, then kills. (js/mp.js:651-688, js/mp.js:830-840, js/mp.js:383-400)
- Ghost part health arrives as 'hp' rows [id, pct] every 500 ms (onHp), so the shooter can see the target's part hp fraction. (js/mp.js:640-646, js/mp.js:824-829)
- Target 3D models (render3d targetModel: tank is a hull box, tracks, turret box and barrel). syncCombat only wiggles the AA turret while it fires; nothing aims the target models. Tracers are a line buffer; missiles, rockets and bombs are pooled meshes. (js/render3d.js:3095-3110, js/render3d.js:3112-3169)
- The flight camera is an orbit chase cam (fc.yaw/pitch/follow) in render3d. There is no aim ray or unproject; only Render3D.project(ax,ay,az) exists. Fire input is KeyV or touchFire (F.firing) plus F.fireTap. (js/render3d.js:2346, js/render3d.js:2380-2394, js/render3d.js:2412-2418, js/render3d.js:3320, js/flight.js:605, js/flight.js:986, js/flight.js:1087)
- The test harness exposes combat internals by rewriting the literal '  return {\n    S, step,' at the end of combat.js and listing names (boxesOf, hitVessel, muzzle, explode, fireGun, turretFire, turretStep, ...). The combat baseline fingerprints every plane template plus RAIDER: boxes, muzzles, blast hp, gun rounds, flares, turret rounds. (test/lib/game.js:37-48, test/combat.test.js:358-394)
- Ground parts today: cab (control 2, crew 2), chassis, chassisL, carWheel (track 2). The only car template is the Dune Buggy. VEHICLE_KINDS.car's tray categories already include 'weapons'. (js/parts.js:422-430, js/parts.js:25-30, js/builder.js:136-139)

## Constraints
- Old designs must fight exactly as before. The 'turret' and 'turretL' parts must keep auto-AA plus the PR #37 grounded hold (combat.js:356-359), so a player-aimed gun needs a new kind or flag, never changed behaviour on existing defs.
- def.armor on fuselageArmor is a 0.6 damage fraction applied in damage() (combat.js:47). A mm-based armour model must use a new field and must not change it.
- test/combat.test.js fingerprints every plane template plus RAIDER (boxesOf, muzzle, explode blast hp, fireGun rounds, flares, turretFire rounds) against test/baseline/combat.json. Changing these functions for existing parts moves the baseline.
- test/lib/game.js:40 requires the literal '  return {\n    S, step,' at the end of combat.js and regex-finds `function name` / `const name =` for the exposed internals. Keep that shape and add new internals to its names list.
- Combat.damage is a no-op on v.remote. All damage to another player goes through MP.sendHit/sendHits (capped at 64 rows and 5000 per row on receipt, mp.js:860-874), and only between hostile players (MP.hostile).
- No AI, range targets, SAMs or auto-countermeasures in any MP room: mode() is 'off' when mpOn() (combat.js:27-28). Any new AI must check mpOn().
- NET_VERSION (net.js:11) must be bumped for new weapon parts or kinds, new shot kinds (netShot maps unknown k to a rocket, combat.js:826) or hit semantics (a new 'k' in hit messages), and for anything an older peer would build, pose or hit differently.
- Peers are untrusted. Sanitise any new net fields with vec, clamp or Number.isFinite as netShot and onPartVis do. Never put peer text into innerHTML.
- Physics is per substep (h = 1/120 s via Combat.step). New aiming and firing must be frame-rate independent. Frame-rate work belongs in Combat.frame (dt) and must not change the result with the 60 fps cap.
- Hit tests run per shot, per substep, per vessel: hitVessel loops every part's boxes and boxesOf allocates. Penetration with multi-part rays and spall must stay cheap on phones (the owner watches battery and FPS).
- Combat.drawHUD runs only in the 3D path (main.js:101) on the 2D overlay canvas, so new HUD elements must find room on a portrait phone (see rwrPlace) and on desktop.
- Player-visible names must not use real designations (no real tank, ATGM or SAM names), per CLAUDE.md.
- Turret aim already travels at 0.01 rad precision, 5 Hz, only when it changes (mp.js:622-635). Show rounds for a remote turret fire along that aim. Hits are the shooter's to decide, so remote aim lag is cosmetic only.
- Coordinates: for cars, part +x is forward, +y up and +z right (PILOT.car, vessel.js:103). partAxis rot 0 is +y (up), so a forward-firing car gun needs rot 3. A turret's base is its part's +x side, so a car turret on top needs rot 3.

## Risks
- Double-counting armour in multiplayer: if the shooter computes post-penetration damage, the receiver's damage() still applies (1-def.armor) (combat.js:47 via mp.js:874).
- Turret hit boxes don't rotate: boxesOf uses the static partRect (combat.js:82-85), so a traversed turret or a long barrel is hit as its unrotated box. Side and rear armour faces of a turned turret would be wrong unless the boxes or faces are turned by aimYaw.
- The owner is skipped for the first 1.5 s of a shot (combat.js:552). A shell that ricochets back off nearby terrain or the vessel's own parts can't hit the shooter early. Conversely, keep the skip so muzzles inside the hull don't self-hit.
- turretFire spawns rounds at the part centre +1.5 m (combat.js:390), not at the barrel tip. A long tank barrel would fire from inside the turret, and the tracer would start there visibly.
- Bullet drag (1.2e-4·ρ·v²) kills about 100 m/s per second at 900 m/s. Using it for APFSDS at 1700 m/s would bleed heavily and make long shots miss. Shells need their own drag.
- The HUD pipper assumes the hull's nose and only gravity (combat.js:1252-1253). A turret gun or a high-drag shell would make the reticle lie.
- A ground vehicle with weapons is invisible to AI AA, SAMs and fighters (the 'flying' gate at combat.js:1121; tgtOk at 1032), so a hostile solo ground mode does nothing without new AI.
- candidates() treats only kind 'car' or landed ghosts as ground targets (combat.js:459, 462). A new vehicle kind would be invisible to AGMs and would be locked by air seekers when not 'landed'.
- clearLine is Earth-only, samples about every 150 m and needs 4 m clearance (combat.js:730-737). That is too coarse for tank-to-tank LOS behind hills or buildings at short range, and scenery (buildings, trees) is not considered at all.
- Range targets are hit as spheres (combat.js:560-561) with hp only. Under a penetration model, MG rounds would still kill a 'tank' target unless targets gain armour.
- Removing or renaming internals listed in test/lib/game.js:42-45 silently drops them from Combat._t and breaks tests.
- Adding more S.shots kinds without updating render3d syncCombat's pools (render3d.js:3118-3135, used {missile, rocket, bomb}) throws in CB.kinds[kk] for an unknown kind, and App.frame swallows the exception every frame.

## Open questions
- Armour resolution: one mm value per part, or per face (front, side, top, rear by segBox axis and sign)? The axis-aligned box model gives six faces for free.
- Should a tank turret be a single rotating part whose hit box and armour faces rotate with aimYaw, or should the turret's armour stay static and only the gun turn?
- Kill condition for ground vehicles: keep 'control part destroyed' (the cab) as today, or add WT-style crew knockout and ammo-rack detonation (destroyPart ignores gun and shell ammo today)?
- Who computes penetration in multiplayer: the shooter (consistent with today's shooter-authoritative hits) or the victim (would need a new message with impact angle and shell stats)?
- Should the player-aimed gun be a new weapon kind selectable with Tab (systems() includes it) or a separate always-on gunner control? How does it coexist with hull-fixed guns on the same vehicle?
- Aim input on phones: a drag-to-aim on the 3D view (camera follows the gun) versus the current orbit camera drag. Is the camera's look direction the aim, as in WT?
- Should the range's 'tank' and 'truck' targets become real Vessel-based AI (like spawnFighter), so they have parts and armour and shoot back, or stay as spheres with a new armour field?
- Do ATGMs need SACLOS (follow the shooter's aim) in addition to the existing lock-and-forget ground seeker (guide())? Do player SAMs need an all-round search radar (a vessel version of siteSees)?
- Should the existing fixed aircraft guns (cannon, gatling) also get penetration vs the new armour (WT does this for CAS)? That would change the anti-tank feel of current planes, though not old designs' behaviour against old targets if gated.
