===== ground/wp0
SUMMARY: WP0, the Phase 0 safety nets. All of it is test code: nothing in js/, docs or other tests changed. The worktree started on cc0e666 (main), not the skeleton. It had no changes, so I ran `git reset --hard 2e9884a` to start from the skeleton as the brief asked.

1. test/driving.js and test/baseline/driving.json (14 entries, 189 KB)
- Craft are driven the way the game drives them. combat.js is loaded as in the page, so the crash limit is 1.6× breakSpeed and dents happen. The stick is set once per 1/60 s frame. Each frame then runs two 1/120 s substeps, each `v.physicsStep(h, s)` then `Combat.step(h, s)` then `s.time += h`: the order of Flight.physicsTick. The sim carries active, vessels, firing and destroyed.
- Every 0.25 s it records, at toPrecision(17): position from the start, velocity, quaternion, angular velocity, gForce, and the flags landed, wheelsDown, splashed and canDrive. Each scenario also gets a 0.00 s sample, an `end` sample, `liftoff`, `broken` and the part count.
- The baseline is keyed one field per sample, so every differing sample prints as its own DIFF line (check with show: Infinity). Every scenario runs twice in one process; any difference prints FAIL. Word arguments run a filtered set without comparing to the baseline.
- Dune Buggy scenarios:
  - forward: 10 s flat out on the runway, then 4 s coasting;
  - cornering at 10, 20 and 30 m/s: full stick until wheelSpeed reaches V, then the stick at V/38 (the speed controller holds V) with full right steer for 5 s. At 10 m/s it rolls over and is righted;
  - braking: to 20 m/s, then 4 s on the brake;
  - reverse: 8 s, full left steer for the last 3 s;
  - causeway: starts at e 3000 on the ramp and drives 20 s. A player-like lane keeper steers toward z = 0 and an easterly heading, because left alone it drifts off the 14 m road;
  - Shetland slope: e 17720, z -620, yaw 0.0089 (found by searching the real map; the ground rises 9.85° over 60 m, 9.2-10.6° at 4 m baselines). 6 s uphill, then 4 s parked on it;
  - sea: added beyond the brief, to cover water drag. It starts at the bay.
- Dragonfly: on the helipad with throttle 0 (as Flight.start sets it), stick forward, steering after 3 s.
- Lunar Rover: it is a Moon rover. Both separators are fired with cutSeparator and splitComponents, as Flight.fireSeparator does, minus the random tumble. The lander (2 Kolibri engines, 2 Rover Wheels, Wide Tank, Probe Core L) is set on the Moon's track at angle 0.2592 (under 0.3° over 80 m) and drives 6 s.
- Falcon, Mustang and FS-16 take-off rolls: us4.test.js's take-off script (staged, throttle 1, attitude hold, full back stick above 1.15× the stall speed while pitch is under 10°), run until 4 s after lift-off. Lift-off times: 6.68 s, 7.88 s and 6.65 s.
- Mutation checks (scratch copies run with ROOT=): the plane-wheel rolling friction (0.025 to 0.026), which no suite caught before, now moves all three take-offs. The steer limit, drive gain, coast-down, water drag, water side grip and righting torque ea
DEVIATIONS:
