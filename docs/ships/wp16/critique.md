# WP16 critique (sceptic): defects in design.md / briefs-wp16.md, checked against wp16/tree (727e2d2)
Probes: `wp16/sceptic/probe1-4*.js` (node, the tree's own parts.js/ship.js). Severity: blocker / major / minor.

1. **[blocker] Floating parts: roof kit "inside the box" lowers roofs under the parts that stand on them.** bridgeM's body is 2.6 m in its 3 m box (§3.3) and every superstructure part gets roof rails "inside the part's box" (§3.1), so the visible roof sits 0.4-1 m under the box top, where other parts' feet are. Osca/Pegazus: bridgeM top 5.0, mastPole foot 5.0, nRadarFC foot 5.0, so both float 0.4 m. Arley/Olivar: funnels and CIWS stand at the deckhouse/hangar box tops (10.5, 11.5), mast and radars at the bridge top (12.5). Fix: roofs at the box top. Roof rails and searchlights only on a roof edge no part stands on (a per-part neighbour mask in ctx, see 4), never by lowering the roof. Add a 3e check that every part resting on another meets drawn geometry within 2 cm.
2. **[blocker] The hull colour pass can't meet its own budgets.** A Sutherland-Hodgman cut of `shullTris` with §4's grid, 5 cm seams, frames and boot-top (probe4b, grid aligned to the rings, no glyphs or streaks yet) gives X 8.3-9.1 k a segment (budget 6 k), L 6.2-6.8 k (4 k), M 3.4-5.2 k (2.5 k), S 1.8 k (1.2 k). That is about 51 k for the Arley's hull alone. With the grid only, no seams, it is still ~28 k, matching §10's own "~30 k". That breaks T2 ≤ 25 k for the whole ship, and this hull casts into the shadow map. Without the uniform grid, seams+frames+boot-top come to 4.1-5.0 k on an X segment (probe4d). Fix: drop the uniform grid; take plate tones per plate from the seam and frame cuts. Make the hull's `mid` today's band loft (~1.2 k a segment), which casts. Give `near` the cut-in loft (indexed, ≤ 5 k), swapped at T1, never drawn together. A shadow-only coarse copy (§10's fallback) needs the city's count-0 stand-in swap (CLAUDE.md "Shadow-only casters"); a shadow-only layer won't work.
3. **[major] The tiers miss the owner's real views.** On a phone in portrait the Arley's follow camera stands `Ship.chaseFar(v,390,844)` = 466 m, so k = 905/(466−81) = 2.35 px/m: T2 on High (on at ≥ 3), and on Low (≥ 5) too. Her aim camera (195 m) gives 7.9 px/m (phone) and 6.8 (720p): T1. So on the destroyer the owner sees mid only: boats (near) leave empty 1.5 m-deep bays, and rafts, doors, glass, ESM domes and yards are gone. The "30 m" list never shows in either default view (probe3). §1's "200 m chase" picture is the desktop's. Fix: class pieces by their size at these views (boats, raft racks, glass band, yards, domes → mid), or T1 on at 2 px/m on High. No recess in mid without what fills it. Add `phone-arley-chase` (DPR 1.75) with the tier logged to §9 and to the bench rows.
4. **[major] Per-def superstructure ignores its neighbours.** Olivar: bridge 21..31, deckhouses 7..21 and −7..7 butt end to end (the Perry's one long block), but each model carries its own roof-edge rails, coamings, end-wall doors and ladders. That puts rail fences across one continuous roof at x 7 and 21. Every deckhouse also gets a boat bay a side, so the Olivar and the Arley each carry four identical RHIBs (the real ships two). Fix: a ctx neighbour mask per part (fore/aft/sides/top blocked; `CTX_USE.house/bridgeL/hangar: ['nb']`, ≤ 16 variants, cache-friendly). Boats only on the deckhouse nearest amidships (ctx `bay`), or move edge fittings into a per-vessel superstructure dressing culled like the hull's (WP16c).
5. **[major] Hollowed models break "what is hit is what is drawn" for superstructure.** Kit hits are the box (armour.js:229 `d.outline || BOX_OUTLINE`; no ship kit has an outline). Today's models fill the box. The plan leaves air inside it: the Bridge's pilot house is 7.5 of 12 m across over its top 3 of 7 m (≈ 20 % of the box), aegis walls lean 8° (0.7 m in at a deckhouse top), stealth walls slope 10°, boat bays are 1.5 m deep. Shells then burst in the air beside the pilot house. Fix: bound it in 3e: each box face ≥ 85 % covered by surface within 0.3 m (a coverage raster), bays ≤ 0.6 m. Otherwise tell the owner it is a looks-only exception.
6. **[major] Cut-in colour aliases like geometry and gets no mip filtering.** 5 cm seams, draft-mark bars, panel grids and glyph strokes are sliver triangles. At T2 (0.7-3 px/m) a seam is 0.04-0.15 px and a 1.5 m number's stroke under a pixel. They crawl as the ship rolls, so "read crisply at every distance" (§2) is false. Fix: a minimum width per tier (≥ 1 px at the tier's entry k). Seams, numbers and marks go only in the near loft of item 2, and seams become tone steps between plates (one cut, not a dark 5 cm strip).
7. **[major] Model builds and dressing are synchronous at first sight, mid-battle.** syncVessels builds a VesselMesh at first sight (render3d.js:6255) for every ship within 50 km. That includes buildPart→model() for each part, the ctx-keyed bow (40 ms desktop ≈ 160 ms on 4× phone) and stern, and shipDress (15/60 ms), even for ships only ever seen at T3. Hostile waves every 30 s and Practice/battle respawns (ship.js NRANGE) bring ships in during play, so §6.5's "longest frame ≤ base + 16 ms while a battle spawns" can't hold. Fix: build ctx lofts and dressing in `runJobs` when a ship first reaches T2, behind the plain loft. Cut labels into a small label patch (only the triangles the glyph boxes touch), not by re-cutting the whole segment.
8. **[major] ctx text: leaks and peers disagree.** `models` (parts3d.js:8) is never evicted, so each distinct number or name keeps a whole non-indexed X bow or stern (~6 k tris ≈ 0.8 MB). Peers: `vesselOf`/`buildGhost` set `v.name = nameOf(pid)` (mp.js:1470-1481), the player's nickname, not the spawn message's design name. The transom therefore differs per screen, and free peer text gets geometry. The number hashes positions, but spawnRows rounds them to mm (mp.js:1135), so screens can differ. Every AI ship of a template also shares one number. Fix: hash types + positions quantised to 0.1 m, sorted, salted per AI slot. Peers show the design name from the spawn message (store it on the ghost; an mp.js line owned by a1) or no name. Label patches with an LRU.
9. **[major] The bake vs tiers.** bakeList skips `!o.visible` (parts3d.js:2863). A wreck baked at T2/T3 (it bakes 6 s after wrecking, at whatever distance) loses rigs the tier hid (guns, radars, CIWS, screws). And "bake takes mid" makes near/fine vanish 6 s into the end camera on the player's own wreck. Fix: the bake chars the merged layers and keeps them, folding only rigs into one mesh, and walks rigs whatever the tier.
10. **[major] The budgets don't add up, and one row is unmeetable.** Summing the briefs' per-part maxima for the Arley: hull 36 k + bridge 12 + 2×7 + mast 6 + 2×3 + helideck 3 + screws and rudders 10 + arms ≈ 33 + dressing 25 ≈ 148 k, over T0 ≤ 140 k and well over T1 ≤ 110 k. T1 shadow draws: mid + gun (3 nodes) + house + CIWS (3) + radars (4 nodes) = 12 > 10. Fleet row "canvas draws ≤ 80": look.md's own fit gives ~68 draws for sea and sky, and the Arley alone is allowed 26 at T0. The non-ship base was never measured. Fix: allocate each tier's triangles and draws to b/c/d so the sums fit; measure `fleet` with the ships hidden before setting its row.
11. **[major] The merge's integration has no owner.** a2 is validated on today's models only. b/c/d content first meets it in WP16e: rig.dress, classes, glass in near, per-part camo offsets (`camoMap(S, at)` offsets by p.y, parts3d.js:2142; a merged camo layer must bake that into its UVs or the pattern jumps), caps. And e "must not fix other packages' code". The brief's "draws ≤ 22 at T1 already holds with today's models" is false: today's Arley has 19 material groups, 7 of them per-type textures that can't share a material (bridgeL, deckhouseL, funnel, helipad, nRingM, nHouse127, vls8), plus ~17 rig meshes ≈ 30. Fix: add a3 after d, owned by a, re-running every a2 check on the final models; reword a2's done-criteria to "report".
12. **[major] Defining features missing (real.md).** The Arley's raked tripod mast (her 1.5 km item 2) stays a 4-leg Perry lattice: add `aegis` to mastLattice's CTX_USE (three raked legs). The Mk 92 egg radome (Olivar 1.5 km item 3, the Pegazus' "white egg") is missing, and no per-instance variant can exist with a per-def cache: give ctx an nth-instance or role field. The Arley's "one big forward block" has a 2 m gap between bridge (37..47) and deckhouse (21..35): close it in §7a or drop the checklist item. The Vizby keeps exposed triple tubes (x −13.7) and SRBOC (−32.6) against "absence of clutter": add nvTorp/nvDecoy to stealth's CTX_USE.
13. **[major] nRadarAir can't be "the big rectangular antenna".** Its box is 4 × 1.2 × 1.2 m (w/h fixed), and the pedestal must share the 1.2 m, so the reflector is ≤ 0.8 m tall: about 3 × 0.6 px at 1.5 km (Olivar checklist). Fix: the mast's top carries the pedestal and gearbox, so the reflector fills its box. Re-word the checklist to what the box allows.
14. **[minor] The cut projections are wrong.** §4 cuts "in the side projection (x, y)". A transom (x ≈ const) projects to a line, so the transom name can't be cut that way. Deck fields, the waterway margin and hatch outlines need plan (x, z). One glyph set in (x, y) cuts both sides alike, so the port number reads mirrored. Fix: project per face (side by sign of z with mirrored glyphs on port, plan for code −6, end view for caps).
15. **[minor] Colour per vertex position bleeds.** "Each vertex coloured by one function of (x, y, z, …)" (§4) is ambiguous on a cut line: a seam or glyph colour smears across the whole neighbouring piece. Today's shipHullGeo colours per emitted piece (`emit`, parts3d.js:806-839). Fix: a region id per piece, with the gradient on top.
16. **[minor] The RIB collar breaks 3f.** ribHull's deck half-breadth reaches 1.6 = depth/2. A Ø 0.6 m tube centred on the deck edge reaches z 1.9 > depth/2 + 1.5 cm. The 1.3 m above-deck cap and box-bound whips also make the RIB's arch, the ensign and jack staffs and the whips stubby (real ones are 2-10 m). Fix: exempt `tubes` explicitly in 3f; add a `tall` thin-rod class (≤ 5 cm Ø) allowed past the box or the cap, never picked or hit, listed as a 3e exemption.
17. **[minor] Sub-pixel rails.** Lifelines Ø 2.5 cm at T0's 12 px/m are 0.3 css px (0.5 device px at High's DPR cap 1.75, render3d.js:7774). MSAA (antialias: true) still leaves them crawling, so the brief's "no shimmer at 60 m" fails. Fix: game-thick rails (stanchions 6 cm, lifelines 4 cm) or a fine threshold from the thinnest piece (≥ 0.8 device px).
18. **[minor] Detail spent where nobody sees it.** 5 mm porthole rims and ≤ 1.4 cm bolts, lifting eyes and strake strips are classed near (drawn from ~300 m at 0.02-0.05 px; near is defined as ≥ 0.2 m), where they only sparkle. The 8 inner parts get V-banks, turbos and injector rails, but X-ray multiplies their vertex colours by the GV_MOD tint (xrayRig), so dark enamel reads black. Fix: drop sub-cm relief (colour only); keep inner parts tidy with light (≥ 0.7) vertex colours.
19. **[minor] Hangar.** fade() resets castShadow on every mesh by rig.small only (parts3d.js:2660), so after any X-ray or loose-part fade, near/fine/glass cast shadows again. X-ray turns rails, nets and louvres into 0.3-opacity transparent clutter. Unmerged, the Arley's ~4 meshes a part make ~150-170 meshes (×2 with PCF_SOFT) when orbiting on a phone (today 72), with no bench row. Fix: fade honours `userData.lod`; X-ray hides near/fine; the builder merges classes per part (shipVC + glass); add a hangar-ship bench row.
20. **[minor] Far default loses the 1.5 km cues.** A part's far silhouette is its box "in their mean colour", so a funnel's black top averages into grey. At 1.5 km a phone is at 0.64 px/m and 720p at 0.54 (T3), and Low is always T3. Fix: the far default keeps the top band's own colour (split the box), with explicit `far` on funnel and mast.
21. **[minor] Split, memory, upload.** A ship falling in two recomputes ctx for the new half: the bow's number changes, and an uncached bow builds at that moment (inherit ctx). Near/fine layers are never freed, against "≤ 20 MB for six ships" (free them after 30 s at T2+). Collapsing runs keeps CPU arrays (index the layers and collapse indices). The first draw uploads a 2-6 MB layer in one frame; the 3 ms slice covers only JS (upload in pieces, or warm during a T2 frame).
22. **[minor] Unverifiable criteria.** gpu ≤ 1.05-1.10× is below SwiftShader's ±10 % noise (look.md). A "pixel-equal merge ≤ 0.01 %" between two frames fails on the animated swell and wake: render one paused frame twice, merge toggled. Phone shots at device scale 1 understate aliasing (the game uses 1.75). "z-fight ≤ noise" is undefined. Fix: name the hard checks (draws, shadow draws, programs, vertices, longest frame) and give numbers.
23. **[minor] Ownership gaps.** a, b, c and d all edit the one `CTX_USE` literal (each region should `Object.assign(CTX_USE, …)`). The shipDress stub is in a's region but implemented in b's (put the stub in b's region from a1). The hangar call (render3d.js:7601) needs `name: Builder.name` and has no owner, and a rename never rebuilds the transom (give it to a2). State "no DoubleSide on ship kinds" (nets, flags, mesh boards); DoubleSide is a new program variant.
